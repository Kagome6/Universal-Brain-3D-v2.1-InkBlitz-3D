## 5. Online Lagged Causal Integration Diagnostics ($\Phi_G$)

### 5.1 Why Equal-Time Correlation Fails as an Integration Metric

When monitoring whether multiple cortical modules operate as an integrated whole or as disconnected parallel channels, naive equal-time mutual information $I(X_{M_1}^t ; X_{M_2}^t)$ produces severe false positives: if an external environmental flash (such as an explosion or sudden arena event) simultaneously drives visual, spatial, and somatic inputs, equal-time correlation spikes even when zero directed causal links exist between the modules [@barrett2011practical; @barnett2014mvgc; @mediano2019beyond].

To measure genuine **cross-module causal information flow** at runtime ($12\text{ Hz}$) without stalling control, `ConsciousGWTProcess` implements the information-geometric formulation of lagged integrated information $\Phi_G$ introduced by Oizumi, Tsuchiya, and Amari [-@oizumi2016unified].

### 5.2 Macro-Variable Extraction and Zero-Leakage Off-Diagonal Ridge VAR

At each cognitive cycle $t$, `extract_5module_activity_vec` summarizes the instantaneous state of the five workspace specialists into a 5D macro-state $\mathbf{x}_t = [x_{P,t}, x_{I,t}, x_{W,t}, x_{M,t}, x_{S,t}]^\top \in \mathbb{R}^5$, representing Perceptual energy/surprise ($P$), Allocentric spatial/MEC salience ($I$), World-model KL surprise and counterfactual spread ($W$), Somatic hazard/resource tension and top-down modulation ($M$), and Self-model prediction error ($S$). Over a rolling window of length $T \le 48$ ($K = T - 1$ transition pairs $(\mathbf{x}_{t-1}, \mathbf{x}_t)$ weighted by mild exponential recency $w_k \propto 0.98^{K-1-k}$ with Kish effective sample size $N_{\mathrm{eff}} = 1 / \sum_k w_k^2$), each channel is standardized and multiplied by a soft variance gate $g_{\mathrm{var}, i} = \mathrm{clip}(\sigma_i / 0.08, 0, 1)$ so dormant near-constant channels do not amplify numerical noise:

$$\tilde{x}_{i,t} = \frac{x_{i,t} - \mu_i}{\max(\sigma_i, 10^{-5})} \cdot \mathrm{clip}\!\left(\frac{\sigma_i}{0.08},\, 0,\, 1\right). \tag{12}$$

A subtle pitfall arises if the full linear vector autoregressive (VAR) transition matrix $\mathbf{A} \in \mathbb{R}^{5\times 5}$ ($\tilde{\mathbf{x}}_t = \mathbf{A}\tilde{\mathbf{x}}_{t-1} + \boldsymbol{\varepsilon}_t$) is estimated with standard isotropic ridge regression $(\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_{XX} + \lambda \mathbf{I}_5)^{-1}$: when modules share strong equal-time common input ($\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_{XX}$ has large off-diagonal entries) while true causal dynamics are purely diagonal ($\mathbf{A}_{\mathrm{true}} = \mathrm{diag}(a_1, \dots, a_5)$), shrinking diagonal self-weights leaks self-autocorrelation into nonzero off-diagonal cross-weights $\hat{A}_{ij} \neq 0$, creating spurious $\Phi_G > 0$.

We eliminate diagonal-null common-input leakage via **Row-Wise Off-Diagonal Ridge Estimation**: for each target module $i \in \{0, \dots, 4\}$, we penalize *only* cross-module incoming weights ($j \neq i$), leaving self-persistence $A_{ii}$ unshrunk (`lam_off = 0.14`, `eps_diag = 1e-3`):

$$\hat{\mathbf{a}}_{i,:} = \boldsymbol{\Sigma}_{YX}[i, :] \left( \boldsymbol{\Sigma}_{XX} + \lambda_{\mathrm{off}}(\mathbf{I}_5 - \mathbf{e}_i\mathbf{e}_i^\top) + \varepsilon_{\mathrm{diag}}\mathbf{I}_5 \right)^{-1}. \tag{13}$$

> **Proposition 3 (Population Zero Common-Input Leakage Under Off-Diagonal Ridge VAR):** Suppose the 5 modules have zero cross-module causal links ($\mathbf{A}_{\mathrm{true}} = \mathrm{diag}(a_1, \dots, a_5)$) while receiving arbitrarily correlated equal-time external input such that population covariance $\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_{XX} \succ 0$ has dense off-diagonal terms and population lag-1 cross-covariance satisfies $\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_{YX} = \mathbf{A}_{\mathrm{true}}\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_{XX}$. Under standard isotropic ridge ($\lambda\mathbf{I}_5$), $\hat{\mathbf{a}}_{i,:} = a_i \mathbf{e}_i^\top \boldsymbol{\Sigma}_{XX}(\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_{XX} + \lambda\mathbf{I}_5)^{-1}$ acquires nonzero off-diagonal entries $\hat{A}_{ij} \neq 0$ whenever $\Sigma_{XX,ij} \neq 0$, inducing false-positive $\Phi_G > 0$. Under Eq. (13), because $\mathbf{e}_i^\top (\mathbf{I}_5 - \mathbf{e}_i\mathbf{e}_i^\top) = \mathbf{0}^\top$, we have the algebraic identity $a_i \mathbf{e}_i^\top \left(\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_{XX} + \lambda_{\mathrm{off}}(\mathbf{I}_5 - \mathbf{e}_i\mathbf{e}_i^\top)\right) = a_i \mathbf{e}_i^\top \boldsymbol{\Sigma}_{XX} = \boldsymbol{\Sigma}_{YX}[i, :]$. Multiplying on the right by the regularized inverse yields $\hat{\mathbf{a}}_{i,:} = a_i \mathbf{e}_i^\top + \mathcal{O}(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{diag}})$, so $\hat{A}_{ij} = \mathcal{O}(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{diag}})$ ($j \neq i$) for all $\lambda_{\mathrm{off}} > 0$ at population level; combined with finite-sample degrees-of-freedom debiasing in Eq. (15), finite-$T$ realizations concentrate at $\tilde{\Phi}_G = 0.000\text{ bits}$.
>
> *Scope Remark (Multi-Node Block Coupling):* Proposition 3 holds strictly for purely diagonal nulls ($\mathbf{A}_{\mathrm{true}} = \mathrm{diag}(a_i)$). When true dynamics instead form multi-module blocks (e.g., $\mathbf{A}_{\mathrm{true}} = \mathrm{diag}(\mathbf{B}_{2\times 2}, \mathbf{B}_{3\times 3})$ with nonzero internal coupling $A_{ik} \neq 0$ for $i \neq k$ inside the same block), Eq. (13) shrinks within-block off-diagonal entries alongside cross-block entries, mildly attenuating block-cut $\Phi_G$ estimates (`0.070` vs. `0.088 bits` in Section 6.5) as the deliberate bias–variance price of zero false-positive leakage on short $T = 48$ windows.

### 5.3 Majorization–Minimization $m$-Projection Across All 15 Bipartitions

For any nontrivial bipartition $\pi = (M_1, M_2)$ of $\{P, I, W, M, S\}$ ($2^{5-1} - 1 = 15$ candidate cuts), the disconnected manifold $\mathcal{M}_G(\pi)$ severs *only* directed cross-partition transitions ($A'_{M_1 M_2} = \mathbf{0}$, $A'_{M_2 M_1} = \mathbf{0}$) while leaving past marginal $\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_{XX}$ and equal-time residual covariance $\boldsymbol{\Sigma}'_E$ unconstrained [@oizumi2016unified]:

$$\Phi_G(\pi) = \min_{q \in \mathcal{M}_G(\pi)} D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\left(p(\mathbf{X}^{t-1}, \mathbf{X}^t) \,\|\, q(\mathbf{X}^{t-1}, \mathbf{X}^t)\right) = \min_{\mathbf{A}' \in \mathcal{A}(\pi)} \frac{1}{2} \ln \frac{\det \boldsymbol{\Sigma}'_E(\mathbf{A}')}{\det \boldsymbol{\Sigma}_{E,\mathrm{full}}}, \tag{14}$$

where $\boldsymbol{\Sigma}'_E(\mathbf{A}') = \boldsymbol{\Sigma}_{E,\mathrm{full}} + (\hat{\mathbf{A}} - \mathbf{A}')\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_{XX}(\hat{\mathbf{A}} - \mathbf{A}')^\top$. Because $\boldsymbol{\Sigma}'_E$ couples blocks $M_1$ and $M_2$ whenever equal-time residuals correlate, naive block-OLS is suboptimal; we perform 4 monotonic Majorization–Minimization (`MM`) alternating updates solving the precision-coupled normal equations for $\mathbf{A}'_{M_1 M_1}$ and $\mathbf{A}'_{M_2 M_2}$ at $\approx 0.94\text{ ms}$ per bipartition ($\mathrm{p50} = 14.02\text{ ms}$ across all 15 cuts on CPU; Table 2).

To prevent finite-sample noise floor inflation and asymmetric cut bias (`1 vs. 4` cuts trivially severing fewer directed edges than `2 vs. 3` community cuts), we subtract a shrinkage-calibrated degrees-of-freedom baseline ($0.28\,d_{\mathrm{cut}} / N_{\mathrm{eff}}$ with $d_{\mathrm{cut}} = 2|M_1||M_2|$, tuned below the unregularized asymptotic Wilks factor $0.50$ to match $\lambda_{\mathrm{off}} = 0.14$ shrinkage) followed by smaller-block size normalization $K(\pi) = \min(|M_1|, |M_2|)$ [@balduzzi2008integrated; @kitazono2018efficient]:

$$\tilde{\Phi}_G(\pi) = \frac{1}{\ln 2} \max\!\left(0,\, \Phi_G(\pi) - \frac{0.28\,d_{\mathrm{cut}}}{\max(10, N_{\mathrm{eff}})}\right), \qquad \pi^\star = \arg\min_{\pi} \frac{\tilde{\Phi}_G(\pi)}{\min(|M_1|, |M_2|)}. \tag{15}$$

### 5.4 Coupling Causal $\Phi_G$ with Instantaneous Policy-Override Divergence $\Phi_{\mathrm{act}}$

In addition to rolling temporal integration $\tilde{\Phi}_G(\pi^\star)$, `compute_gene_modulated_mip_and_fr` measures instantaneous decision-level integration $\Phi_{\mathrm{act}}$: the minimum forward KL divergence (in bits, corresponding to a 1D reverse $I$-projection / $m$-projection solved via 16 multiplicative EM updates at matched Boltzmann temperature $\tau_{\mathrm{eff}}$) from the integrated `System 2` policy $\boldsymbol{\pi}_{\mathrm{int}}$ onto the convex mixture family $\{ \alpha\,\boldsymbol{\pi}_{\mathrm{S1}} + (1 - \alpha)\mathbf{u}_8 : \alpha \in [0, 1] \}$ spanned by the unmodulated `System 1` reflex policy and the uniform distribution:

$$\Phi_{\mathrm{act}} = \min_{\alpha \in [0, 1]} D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\left(\boldsymbol{\pi}_{\mathrm{int}} \,\|\, \alpha\,\boldsymbol{\pi}_{\mathrm{S1}} + (1 - \alpha)\mathbf{u}_8\right), \qquad \Phi_{\mathrm{total}} = \mathrm{clip}\!\left(\tilde{\Phi}_G(\pi^\star) + 0.25\,\Phi_{\mathrm{act}},\, 0.01,\, 2.50\right). \tag{16}$$

When `System 1` and `System 2` agree during routine uncontested cruising, $\Phi_{\mathrm{act}}$ remains low. When the world-model `Survival Shield` or spatial compass vetoes a hazardous reflex, $\Phi_{\mathrm{act}}$ registers the exact information-geometric magnitude of top-down intervention, exported alongside $\tilde{\Phi}_G(\pi^\star)$ and the 5-bit MIP mask $\pi^\star$ in every `UBNT` telemetry packet.
