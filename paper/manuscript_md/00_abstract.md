## Abstract

Embodied agents in partially observable 3D environments must reconcile $60\text{ Hz}$ sensorimotor control ($\approx 16.7\text{ ms}$) with slower multi-step planning, workspace routing, and offline consolidation, yet naively wiring heterogeneous cognitive modules into a closed loop introduces severe timing, localization, gradient-interference, and action-freezing pathologies. Rather than targeting a task win-rate record or proposing a new standalone layer, this paper specifies a **60 Hz single-GPU cognitive microkernel**, **five cross-module coupling contracts**, and a **falsifiable integration audit** for **Universal Brain 3D (v2.2)**—an asynchronous architecture that couples established primitives (`TT-D3QN`, categorical `RSSM`, `Slot Attention`, a global-workspace module, landmark Kalman localization, `Plan-A` write-isolated inner speech, and `PBT` self-play) across seven OS processes connected by wait-free `SeqLock + CRC32` shared memory, with a single process owning the GPU (`74.1 MB` allocated VRAM for 8 brains, zero `CRC32` errors). We evaluate it in **InkBlitz 3D**, a $180^\circ$-symmetric territory-painting and objective-escort POMDP, using side-swapped multi-seed ablations, held-out interventional simulator rollouts, and a three-criteria integration audit (replaceability, gradient interference, cumulative organ ladder). Under a single-workstation budget (champions trained for 34–71 gradient updates), behavioral results are mixed. Trained champions beat untrained weights (`75.0%`, $p_{\mathrm{Holm}} = 0.011$; three independent training seeds, `71.9% ± 14.3%`) and beat a pure fast-reflex variant (`79.2%`, $p = 0.0005$). Encoder-detached counterfactual training restores the correct escape-over-idle survival ordering on unseen exposed states in `10/10` seeds, at the cost of under-predicting absolute survival. However, the full brain only ties a hand-crafted non-neural scaffold (`50.0%`), loses to a map-ablated opponent in one profile (`18.8%`, $p = 0.0007$), and several modules (workspace competition rule, loop closure, basal-ganglia gain, offline sleep) show no measurable behavioral effect at this budget. We release the microkernel, coupling contracts, arena, and negative-result-inclusive audit protocol as a reproducible systems baseline for testing when assembled cognitive modules genuinely function as one system.
