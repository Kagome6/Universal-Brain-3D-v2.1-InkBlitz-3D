## Abstract

Embodied agents in partially observable 3D environments must reconcile $60\text{ Hz}$ sensorimotor control ($\approx 16.7\text{ ms}$) with slower multi-step planning, workspace routing, and offline consolidation, yet naively wiring heterogeneous cognitive modules into a closed loop introduces severe timing, localization, gradient-interference, saddle-deadlock, and action-freezing pathologies. Rather than targeting a task win-rate record or proposing a new standalone layer, this paper specifies a **60 Hz single-GPU cognitive microkernel**, **five cross-module coupling contracts**, and a **falsifiable integration audit** for **Universal Brain 3D (v2.3.0)**—an asynchronous architecture that couples established primitives (`TT-D3QN`, a 3-head categorical `RSSM`, `Slot Attention`, a 5-specialist global workspace, landmark Kalman localization, `Plan-A` write-isolated inner speech, and `PBT` self-play with progressive `Scaffold Annealing`) across seven OS processes connected by wait-free `SeqLock + CRC32` shared memory, with a single process owning the GPU (`74.1 MB` allocated VRAM for 8 brains, `2.15 ms` `p99` spatial/gating latency, zero `CRC32` errors). We evaluate it in **InkBlitz 3D**, a $180^\circ$-symmetric territory-painting and objective-escort POMDP featuring both open (`Stage 1: Classic Citadel`) and centrally occluded (`Stage 2: Occluded Twin-Flank`) arena geometries, across $K = 3$ independent self-play training runs (`3,440`–`3,472` gradient updates per seed, zero `NaN` rollbacks), side-swapped ablations, held-out interventional rollouts, and a three-criteria integration audit. Operating in 100% autonomous neural mode ($\lambda_{\mathrm{scaf}} = 0.0$, zero heuristic teacher or compass priors), trained champions defeat untrained weights on both stages (`68.8%` on `Stage 1`, $p = 5.7\times 10^{-5}$; `86.1% ± 4.3%` on `Stage 2`, $p < 10^{-6}$), match the full hand-crafted `L2` heuristic on open `Stage 1` (`53.5%`), and **autonomously surpass the `L2` heuristic on occluded `Stage 2` (`61.1% ± 12.9%`, $p = 0.0094$; primary seed `79.2%`, $p = 0.00012$)**. World-model encoder ownership and straight-through zero-gates eliminate shared-overlap gradient conflicts ($\min \cos = -0.055$), reduce held-out depth MAE from `5.88 m` to `1.45 m`, raise lagged causal integration $\tilde{\Phi}_G$ cleanly above untrained and shuffled baselines ($p = 2.4\times 10^{-7}$), and validate the basal-ganglia emergency escape bypass (`81.2%`, $p_{\mathrm{Holm}} = 0.0058$) and workspace broadcast (`68.8%`, $p = 0.049$). At the same time, our audit surfaces sharp negative and null findings: spatial mapping still loses to unmapped direct rushing in the short-clock open generic profile (`20.8%`, $p_{\mathrm{Holm}} = 0.0047$), counterfactual training worsens absolute survival Brier calibration despite restoring `10/10`-seed escape-over-idle ordering, and workspace pooling rules and offline sleep show no detectable win-rate effect. We release the microkernel, coupling contracts, two-stage arena, and negative-result-inclusive audit protocol as a reproducible baseline for testing when assembled cognitive modules genuinely function as one system.
