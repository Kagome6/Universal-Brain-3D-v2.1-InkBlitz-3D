## 7. Related Work

### 7.1 Classical Symbolic, Subsumption, and Neural Cognitive Architectures

Cognitive architectures such as ACT-R [@anderson2004actr], Soar [@laird2012soar], and LIDA [@franklin2014lida] pioneered the decomposition of mind-like control into specialized memory buffers, production rules, attentional codelets, and global broadcasts, while Brooks' Subsumption Architecture [@brooks1986robust] demonstrated how asynchronous fast sensorimotor layers can operate concurrently beneath higher-level competence layers without central clock bottlenecks. LIDA translated Baars' Global Workspace Theory [@baars1988gwt] into a cyclic cognitive loop (`understanding -> consciousness -> action selection`), and Spaun [@eliasmith2012spaun] demonstrated a 2.5-million-neuron spiking brain model performing multiple visual-motor cognitive tasks. However, traditional symbolic architectures rely predominantly on hand-crafted relational tokens, whereas spiking models such as Spaun incur substantial simulation overhead per biological second. Universal Brain 3D shares LIDA's workspace cycle, Subsumption's multi-rate layering, and Spaun's functional regionalization, while instantiating perceptual, predictive, and value modules as compact rate-coded PyTorch tensors operating continuously at 60 Hz over 3D geometry, parabolic ballistic physics, and competitive self-play.

### 7.2 Biophysical Brain Simulations vs. Whole-Brain Reference Architectures

At the biophysical extreme, the Blue Brain / Human Brain Project [@markram2015reconstruction] models cortical columns down to ion-channel conductances and morphological dendrites. While indispensable for cellular neurophysiology, biophysical simulation remains several orders of magnitude too computationally intensive for single-GPU embodied reinforcement learning.

Closer to our macro-design is the Whole Brain Architecture (WBA) framework of Yamakawa [-@yamakawa2021wba], which advocates engineering general intelligence by connecting machine learning modules according to a mesoscopic neuroanatomical blueprint (Whole Brain Reference Architecture, WBRA). We adopt WBA's systems-level division across entorhinal-hippocampal mapping, cerebellar forward kinematics, basal ganglia gating, prefrontal context, and Broca-Wernicke loops. Where Universal Brain 3D (v2.3.0) diverges is methodological priority: rather than constraining every module upfront to strict subnuclear anatomical fidelity (`CA3–CA1`, `Pulvinar`, `TRN`), we prioritize closing a fault-tolerant 60 Hz sensorimotor-to-sleep loop using established deep RL and robotics components first, auditing replaceability, multi-loss gradient orthogonality, autonomous scaffold removal, and cumulative organ trade-offs empirically before introducing finer WBRA circuit motifs.

### 7.3 Deep Reinforcement Learning, World Models, Safe Shielding, and Self-Play Leagues

Inside individual brain regions, our implementation builds on mature reinforcement learning, world-modeling, and representation-learning primitives. Fast candidate valuation combines Double Q-learning [@vanhasselt2016double], Dueling value-advantage decomposition [@wang2016dueling], Prioritized Experience Replay [@schaul2016per], semi-Markov action candidates [@sutton1999options], and Tensor-Train factorized projections [@novikov2015tt]. Multi-step imagination builds on discrete categorical `RSSM` dynamics with unimix priors and world-model encoder ownership from `DreamerV2` and `DreamerV3` [@hafner2021dreamerv2; @hafner2023dreamerv3], spatial grid representations relate to entorhinal navigation codes [@hafting2005grid; @banino2018vector], episodic strategic context uses causal self-attention [@vaswani2017attention], perceptual foreground binding adapts `Slot Attention` [@locatello2020slot], shared inter-module communication relates to deep Global Workspace bottlenecks [@vanrullen2021deep; @goyal2022coordination], and multimodal symbol grounding adapts `VQ-VAE`/`RVQ` [@vandenoord2017vqvae; @zeghidour2021soundstream] and `InfoNCE`/`MIL-NCE` [@vandenoord2018cpc; @miech2020milnce]. Safety arbitration relates to post-shielded reinforcement learning [@alshiekh2018safe], multi-objective gradient isolation relates to multi-task gradient conflict mitigation [@yu2020pcgrad], and continual anchor regularization adapts `L2-SP` [@li2018explicit]. At the population scale, `arena_pbt_trainer.py` combines Population-Based Training in 3D arenas (`FTW`; [@jaderberg2017pbt; @jaderberg2019ftw]), Prioritized Fictitious Self-Play (`AlphaStar`; [@vinyals2019alphastar]), and `DAgger` imitation bootstrap [@ross2011dagger] paired with progressive `Scaffold Annealing` ($\lambda_{\mathrm{scaf}}: 1 \to 0$).

Our contribution relative to this literature is **architectural synthesis, interface contracts, and an integration audit that reports null and negative effects alongside autonomous gains**. First, in `DreamerV3` or `FTW`, a single recurrent or feed-forward policy operates at one uniform step rate without explicit separation between $60\text{ Hz}$ subcortical ballistic/braking loops and $12\text{ Hz}$ workspace deliberation, nor explicit mid-episode respawn map preservation (`flags |= 16`). Second, standard observational world-model training does not address open-space stationary survival confounding or shared-encoder gradient overlap when `System 2` overrides a `System 1` reflex; our world-model encoder ownership, encoder-detached counterfactual exposure loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CF}}$, Eq. 7), and active escape exemption ($\mathrm{esc}_m$, Eq. 8) target these issues directly, with the calibration caveats discussed in Section 6.4. Third, multiplexing 8 competitive whole-brain agents (`~74–76 MB` allocated VRAM) plus Zero-PyTorch C++/NumPy arena workers and 4-phase sleep consolidation (`S-1..S-4`; [@diekelmann2010sleep; @tononi2014shyh; @fedus2020revisiting]) onto a single consumer GPU via `Barrier 1` makes multi-seed architectural audits feasible without cluster-scale compute.

### 7.4 Computational Theories and Information-Theoretic Indicators of Consciousness

Recent consensus reports such as Butlin et al. [-@butlin2023consciousness] evaluate AI architectures against structural indicator properties derived from Global Neuronal Workspace Theory [@baars1988gwt; @dehaene2011experimental; @dehaene2017consciousness], Higher-Order and Attention-Schema Theories [@lau2011hot; @graziano2013social], Predictive Processing [@rao1999predictive; @friston2010fep], and empirical Integrated Information / Granger-causal measures [@balduzzi2008integrated; @barrett2011practical; @barnett2014mvgc; @oizumi2016unified; @kitazono2018efficient; @mediano2019beyond]. Most existing implementations of neural workspaces or $\Phi_G$ estimators are evaluated on static classification tasks, toy gridworlds, or offline time series. Universal Brain 3D embeds a 5-specialist workspace, efference-copy self-monitoring, straight-through zero-gated top-down attentional loops, and live 15-bipartition causal $\Phi_G$ estimation directly inside a 60 Hz 3D adversarial POMDP. This exposes practical issues that only appear in closed-loop operation—such as workspace stagnation without habituation, zero-gate saddle deadlocks, and common-input VAR leakage—while also clarifying the empirical limits of such indicators when live $\tilde{\Phi}_G$ remains near its finite-window detection floor.
