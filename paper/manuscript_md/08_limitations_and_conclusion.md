## 8. Limitations, Scientific Stance, & Conclusion

### 8.1 Current Technical and Biological Limitations

To keep our claims falsifiable and properly scoped, we highlight seven explicit limitations of Universal Brain 3D (v2.3.0):

1. **Seed variance and open-stage parity with hand-crafted heuristics**: Across $K = 3$ independent self-play runs (`3,440`–`3,472` gradient updates per seed), autonomous neural policies ($\lambda_{\mathrm{scaf}} = 0.0$) significantly outperform the full hand-crafted `L2` heuristic on centrally occluded `Stage 2` (`61.1% ± 12.9%`, $p = 0.0094$), yet individual seed strength still varies substantially (`79.2%`, `50.0%`, `54.2%` vs. `L2` on `Stage 2`; `58.3%`, `41.7%`, `60.4%` on `Stage 1`), and pooled performance on open `Stage 1` reaches parity (`53.5%`, $p = 0.384$) rather than superiority. Larger population sizes and multi-tens-of-thousands-update schedules are needed to narrow inter-seed variance.
2. **Several internal rules and offline sleep remain behaviorally neutral at match level**: While zeroing the entire global workspace broadcast degrades win rate (`C7`: `68.8%`, uncorrected $p = 0.049$), replacing learned softmax workspace competition with uniform $1/5$ pooling (`S2`: `77.1%`) or hard one-hot winner-take-all (`S3`: `79.2%`) does not reduce match win rate relative to `L5` (`79.2%`), even though disabling workspace habituation causes severe internal specialist lock-in (`1.39` vs. `4.17` handoffs/s). Likewise, removing single-factor `RSSM` rollout valuation on open `Stage 1` (`C6`: `54.2%`), wiping the map on respawn (`C3`: `47.9%`), or running 4-phase offline sleep (`S-1..S-4`: `45.0%`–`52.5%` vs. no-sleep) produced no statistically significant win-rate gain.
3. **Out-of-distribution open-lane vulnerability of spatial mapping (`C8`)**: While central sightline occlusion on `Stage 2` shifts the map-ablation comparison (`C2b`: `67.7%`, and loop-closure removal `S4` drops `Stage 2` win rate from `79.2%` to `68.8%`), evaluating the `turf_objective`-trained checkpoint zero-shot in the straight-line, open-lane `universal_tactical` profile still loses to a map-wiped sprinter (`C8`: `20.8%`, $p_{\mathrm{Holm}} = 0.0047$). Spatial exploration incentives that help around occluding baffles remain a liability when optimal timeout play reduces to an unoccluded straight-line dash.
4. **Finite-sample noise floor and discrete-grid arena asymmetry**: Self-play controls (`C0`: `54.2%`; `L2` vs. `L2`: `47.9%`) exhibit `95%` confidence intervals spanning roughly $\pm 14$ percentage points at $N = 24$ side-swapped pairs, and the discrete `48x48` grid is not bit-identically $180^\circ$-equivariant within a single unswapped game (Sec. 2.3). Side-swapped pairing cancels fixed spawn offsets in expectation, but subtle `3`–`8` percentage point effects require larger evaluation suites.
5. **Counterfactual probability calibration vs. ordinal ranking**: Encoder-detached $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CF}}$ (Eq. 7) restores the ground-truth escape-over-idle survival ranking on unseen exposed states across `10/10` seeds (`+0.188` gap shift), partly by construction via its `0.12` target penalty, which simultaneously under-predicts absolute survival levels (`0.244`/`0.123` vs. true `0.624`/`0.349`) and worsens the `M7` Brier score (`0.151` vs. `0.117`). Replacing fixed-scalar exposure targets with distributionally calibrated TD-survival bootstrapping is a natural refinement.
6. **Hybrid analytic–neural granularity and single-workstation IPC boundary**: Stationary-landmark scalar Kalman localization (Eq. 4), A* candidate proposal, and 2-iteration ballistic lead-aim remain analytic/C++ subcortical modules rather than end-to-end learned neural circuits, and all `SeqLock` buses rely on single-host shared memory under x86-64 Total Store Order (Sec. 3.2; [@boehm2012seqlock]). Weakly ordered ARM64 hardware or distributed multi-node leagues require explicit acquire/release memory fences and network transport.
7. **Linear-Gaussian macro-variable $\Phi_G$ and default `Plan-A` isolation**: Our online estimator computes linear-Gaussian $\Phi_G$ [@oizumi2016unified] over 5 scalar specialist summaries. Although trained `v2.3.0` dynamics lift $\tilde{\Phi}_G$ cleanly above untrained and shuffled baselines (`9.38` vs. `0.04` and `0.39 × 10^-3 bits`), linear VAR summaries cannot capture higher-order nonlinear synergies, and under `Plan-A` generated Broca inner speech remains read-only telemetry (`0.0` motor bias, while non-verbal RVQ symbol priors carry `0.20` weight).

### 8.2 Scientific Stance on Functionalist Terminology

Throughout this work, terms borrowed from neuroscience and consciousness studies—including *Global Neuronal Workspace*, *Ignition*, *Self-Model*, *Inner Speech*, *Sleep Consolidation*, and *Integrated Information ($\Phi_G$)*—refer strictly to **computational, architectural, and information-theoretic mechanisms** defined by Equations (1)–(17). Following functionalist indicator methodology [@butlin2023consciousness], we make **no claim** that Universal Brain 3D instantiates biological sentience, subjective feelings, or phenomenal experience (*qualia*). Our claim is an engineering and methodological one: that a multi-rate architecture with explicit interface contracts can run these mechanisms concurrently in real time on a single workstation, and that a falsifiable audit can separate genuine cross-module contributions from silent or regime-dependent components.

### 8.3 Future Research Directions

Four extensions follow directly from these findings. First, **long-horizon population scaling** (`50K+` updates with broader map proceduralization) can test whether inter-seed variance narrows and whether `Stage 2`-style multi-route planning transfers zero-shot across unseen obstacle layouts. Second, **calibrated interventional survival learning**—using branched rollouts or distributional survival critics in place of a fixed scalar `0.12` target in Eq. (7)—can preserve `10/10`-seed ordinal safety ranking without sacrificing Brier calibration. Third, **Shadow-to-Zero-Gate WBRA Circuit Refinement** can leverage our straight-through `apply_zero_gate` contract (Eq. 11) to introduce finer Whole Brain Reference Architecture [@yamakawa2021wba] motifs, such as hippocampal `CA3–CA1` pattern completion and thalamic `Pulvinar/TRN` precision routing, without breaking 60 Hz real-time guarantees. Fourth, **End-to-End Retinotopic Stereo Autonomy** can transition primary training from `16x16` raycast foveal patches (`UB3D` v1) to full `64x64x2` binocular RGB disparity (`BinocularRetinotopicDepthNet3D` in `UB3D` v2).

### 8.4 Conclusion

Building a whole-brain agent for real-time 60 Hz control in a partially observable 3D world is fundamentally a problem of **cross-module interface contracts**: preventing asynchronous motor staleness, isolating localization from moving entities, assigning perceptual encoder ownership so TD updates do not corrupt world models, awakening zero-initialized top-down gates without saddle deadlocks, de-confounding counterfactual safety vetoes, and progressively annealing hand-crafted scaffolds ($\lambda_{\mathrm{scaf}}: 1 \to 0$) so learned arbitration can be tested on its own merits. Universal Brain 3D (v2.3.0) realizes these contracts in a 7-process lock-free microkernel on a single consumer GPU (`74.1 MB` VRAM for 8 brains, `2.15 ms` `p99` spatial/gating latency) and demonstrates both autonomous competence—surpassing its full hand-crafted `L2` heuristic on occluded `Stage 2` terrain and cutting 3D world-model depth error fourfold—and sharp empirical boundaries where open-lane rushing, workspace pooling rules, or offline sleep remain negative or neutral. We offer the microkernel, coupling contracts, two-stage `InkBlitz 3D` arena, and negative-result-inclusive audit protocol as a transparent foundation for embodied whole-brain systems research.

### Broader Impact Statement

This work investigates multi-rate cognitive control and functionalist consciousness-indicator diagnostics inside a clean-room, non-violent 3D territory-painting and abstract core-escort game (`InkBlitz 3D`). Two societal considerations merit explicit attention. First, architectures that expose terms such as *Global Workspace Ignition*, *Self-Model Surprise*, *Inner Speech*, and *Integrated Information ($\Phi_G$)* carry a risk of anthropomorphic misinterpretation by non-specialist users. As stated in Section 8.2, these quantities measure mathematical properties of cross-module information routing and predictive error; they imply neither subjective experience nor moral status. Second, while escape-exempt safety shielding (`Survival Shield` and bounded `STN-SNr` braking) suggests design patterns for collision-averse mobile robotics, our policies are trained exclusively in simplified virtual game physics without formal real-world safety certification and must not be deployed on physical autonomous systems without domain-specific verification.
