## 8. Limitations, Scientific Stance, & Conclusion

### 8.1 Current Technical and Biological Limitations

To keep our claims falsifiable and properly scoped, we highlight seven explicit limitations of Universal Brain 3D (v2.2):

1. **Small single-workstation training budget and a weak learned baseline**: Because all league training and `1,200+` evaluation matches were run on one local consumer GPU without cluster compute, the evaluated champions performed only `34`–`71` gradient updates on a few hundred transitions after a `DAgger` bootstrap from a heuristic teacher. The learned components beat untrained weights but, as the cumulative ladder shows (Sec. 6.6), the learned `TT-D3QN` alone loses to the hand-crafted scaffold (`35.4%`) and the full system only reaches parity with it (`50.0%`). Consequently, while real-time latency, VRAM isolation, gradient orthogonality, and exact `Plan-A` invariance are verified regardless of step count, whether multi-thousand-update training activates the currently neutral slow modules or surpasses the scaffold remains an open empirical question.
2. **Several modules show no measurable behavioral effect**: Swapping the workspace competition rule (uniform pooling or hard winner-take-all), replacing loop closure with dead reckoning, or changing basal-ganglia gain left win rate unchanged (`49`–`54%`), and removing the `RSSM` shield (`C6`), the escape bypass (`C5`), or respawn map retention (`C3`) produced no significant difference. Two slow pathways (`L3` top-down slot priors, `L9` strategic context) remained silent in the link scan. These components are plug-compatible, but we cannot currently show that they improve behavior.
3. **The spatial map can hurt timeout outcomes**: Against an opponent whose map is wiped every tick, `Full` loses significantly in the generic profile (`C8`: `18.8%`, $p = 0.0007$) despite more eliminations, and results vary widely across training seeds (`32.8%`–`71.9%`). We hypothesize, but have not verified, that the short unoccluded central lane rewards objective sprinting under the $9\text{ s}$ clock. Benchmark revisions (central sightline cover, longer or progress-decaying timeouts) and trajectory analyses are needed.
4. **Empirical noise floor and arena asymmetry**: Self-play controls deviate from $50\%$ by up to `12.5` points (`C0`: `58.3%`; `L2` vs. `L2`: `62.5%`), and the headless arena is not exactly $180^\circ$-equivariant (Sec. 2.3). Side-swapped pairing cancels fixed spawn advantages in expectation, but effects smaller than this noise floor cannot be resolved with $N = 24$ seed pairs.
5. **Counterfactual calibration and audit scope**: Eq. (7) restores the correct escape-over-idle ordering on held-out states, but partly because its target encodes that ordering, and absolute survival is under-predicted (`0.244`/`0.123` vs. true `0.624`/`0.349`; worse Brier score). The gradient-interference audit reproduces the engine's loss terms in a separate script instead of instrumenting the production training step.
6. **Hybrid analytic–neural granularity and single-workstation boundary**: Stationary-landmark scalar Kalman localization (Eq. 4) and 2-iteration ballistic lead-aim remain analytic, and all `SeqLock` buses rely on local shared memory and x86-64 Total Store Order (Sec. 3.2; [@boehm2012seqlock]). Weakly ordered architectures or multi-host scaling would need explicit memory fences and network transport.
7. **Linear-Gaussian macro-variable $\Phi_G$ and default `Plan-A` isolation**: Our online estimator evaluates linear-Gaussian $\Phi_G$ [@oizumi2016unified] over 5 scalar specialist summaries. It suppresses diagonal-null false positives but shrinks within-block couplings, and live values stay near the detection floor. `Plan-A` removes Broca-token influence on motor values but keeps a `0.15`-weighted RVQ symbol prior, and inner speech is otherwise read-only telemetry.

### 8.2 Scientific Stance on Functionalist Terminology

Throughout this work, terms borrowed from neuroscience and consciousness studies—including *Global Neuronal Workspace*, *Ignition*, *Self-Model*, *Inner Speech*, *Sleep Consolidation*, and *Integrated Information ($\Phi_G$)*—refer strictly to **computational, architectural, and information-theoretic mechanisms** defined by Equations (1)–(16). Following functionalist indicator methodology [@butlin2023consciousness], we make **no claim** that Universal Brain 3D instantiates biological sentience, subjective feelings, or phenomenal experience (*qualia*). Our claim is an engineering one: that a multi-rate architecture with explicit interface contracts can run these mechanisms together in real time on a single workstation, and that a systematic audit can reveal which of them currently affect behavior and which do not.

### 8.3 Future Research Directions

Four extensions follow from these results. First, **longer training with the same audit** (multi-hundred-generation leagues and larger replay budgets) is needed to test whether the silent or behaviorally neutral components (`L3`, `L9`, workspace competition, `RSSM` shield) become load-bearing, and whether the learned system can surpass its scaffold. Second, **benchmark revisions**—central sightline occlusion, progress decay for dropped objectives, and trajectory-level analyses—are needed to understand the map-ablation reversal. Third, **Shadow-to-Zero-Gate WBRA Circuit Refinement** can introduce finer Whole Brain Reference Architecture [@yamakawa2021wba] motifs, such as hippocampal `CA3–CA1` pattern completion and thalamic `Pulvinar/TRN` routing, first as non-interfering shadow predictors and then through zero-initialized residual gates. Fourth, **End-to-End Retinotopic Stereo Autonomy** can replace `16x16` raycast foveal sampling (`UB3D` v1) with calibrated `64x64x2` binocular RGB disparity (`BinocularRetinotopicDepthNet3D` in `UB3D` v2).

### 8.4 Conclusion

Building a whole-brain agent for real-time 60 Hz control in a partially observable 3D world requires solving the **interfaces between organs**: temporal staleness, localization filtering, gradient isolation between objectives, counterfactual de-confounding, target-gated inhibition, and workspace habituation. Universal Brain 3D (v2.2) implements these contracts in a 7-process lock-free microkernel on one consumer GPU and meets its real-time and isolation invariants. Our audit, however, shows that assembling the modules is not the same as making each of them useful: at the current training budget, the full system only matches a hand-crafted scaffold, its spatial map does not improve timeout outcomes, and several modules have no detectable behavioral effect. We offer the 60 Hz microkernel, the five coupling contracts, the arena, and the negative-result-inclusive audit protocol—rather than a claim of competitive task supremacy—as a reproducible baseline for testing when integrated cognitive modules begin to function as one system.

### Broader Impact Statement

This work investigates multi-rate cognitive control and functionalist consciousness-indicator diagnostics inside a clean-room, non-violent 3D territory-painting and abstract core-escort game (`InkBlitz 3D`). Two societal considerations merit explicit attention. First, architectures that expose terms such as *Global Workspace Ignition*, *Self-Model Surprise*, *Inner Speech*, and *Integrated Information ($\Phi_G$)* carry a risk of anthropomorphic misinterpretation by non-specialist users. As stated in Section 8.2, these quantities measure mathematical properties of cross-module information routing and predictive error; they imply neither subjective experience nor moral status. Second, while escape-exempt safety shielding (`Survival Shield` and bounded `STN-SNr` braking) suggests design patterns for collision-averse mobile robotics, our policies are trained exclusively in simplified virtual game physics without formal real-world safety certification and must not be deployed on physical autonomous systems without domain-specific verification.
