## 2. Problem Formulation & The `InkBlitz 3D` POMDP Testbed

### 2.1 Strictly First-Person 3D POMDP Formulation

We formulate embodied tactical control as a continuous-space, multi-rate Partially Observable Markov Decision Process (POMDP) $\mathcal{M} = (\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{A}, \mathcal{O}, T, Z, R, \gamma)$ operating at a base physics step $\Delta t = \frac{1}{60}\text{ s}$ inside a bounded 3D arena $\mathcal{W} = [x_{\min}, x_{\max}] \times [y_{\min}, y_{\max}] \times [z_{\min}, z_{\max}]$ (configured at $[-12, +12] \times [0, 6] \times [-16, +16]\text{ m}$ in `Stage 1` and $[-14, +14] \times [0, 6] \times [-18, +18]\text{ m}$ in `Stage 2` within the headless benchmark `Brain3DConfig`, and parameterized dynamically via `UB3D` headers for full-scale Unity 6 stage geometries).

Unlike omniscient game benchmarks that expose global state tensors or wall-penetrating coordinates, the observation function $Z(s_t) = o_t$ enforces four physical first-person partial-observability constraints:

1. **Frustum and Raycast Line-of-Sight (LOS) Culling**: Semantic entities $e_i \in \mathbb{R}^{10}$ (`[id, x, y, z, vx, vy, vz, radius, tag, confidence]`, up to $N_{\max} = 48$, including visible incoming enemy projectiles `id = 500 + k`) are emitted *only* if they lie within range $R_{\max} = 16.0\text{ m}$, satisfy camera half-angle $\theta_{\mathrm{fov}} = 38^\circ$ around current gaze $(\psi_{\mathrm{gaze}}, \theta_{\mathrm{gaze}})$ (or immediate proximity $< 2.3\text{ m}$), and pass obstacle segment-occlusion raycasts. Fixed strategic landmarks known by game rules (friendly and enemy goal pedestals) remain semantically addressable anchors.
2. **Surface-Coupled Stealth Disappearance**: Floor state is represented by a spatial field $C_t: \mathbb{R}^2 \to [-1, +1]$ encoding team territory (`+1` friendly paint, `0` neutral, `-1` opponent paint). When an opponent submerges into its own territory ($C_t \cdot \mathrm{team}_{\mathrm{opp}} > 0.28$) in cover refuge beyond $3.6\text{ m}$, it is **excluded from both the visible entity list and 1st-person ray silhouettes**; foveal rays pass through the hidden collider to sample the underlying floor.
3. **Event-Gated First-Person Acoustics and Non-Omniscient Somatic Hazard**: Both Unity 6 (`ArenaAudioSystem.cs`) and the headless arenas (`HeadToHeadTactical3DArena`, `StandaloneTactical3DArena`) generate directional acoustic cues $(\sin\phi_{\mathrm{aud}}, \cos\phi_{\mathrm{aud}}, \ell_{\mathrm{aud}})$ strictly from audible physical events: weapon discharge ($\ell_0 = 0.92(1 - d/15)_+$), unsubmerged locomotion above $0.55\text{ m/s}$ ($\ell_0 = 0.62(1 - d/11)_+$), or fast close-range splash ($> 0.80\text{ m/s}$ within $4.2\text{ m}$), attenuated by $\times 0.52$ across occluding cover. Whenever attenuated loudness falls below $\ell_{\mathrm{aud}} < 0.05$ (such as a stationary or slow-swimming opponent), directional bearing resets identically to the uninformative neutral vector $(0, 1, 0)$. Likewise, scalar `hazard_level` is computed exclusively from somatic health damage, underfoot enemy paint, acoustic loudness, visible unoccluded threats, and incoming visible projectiles—never from unseen ground-truth opponent distance.
4. **Zero True-Pose Leakage (`project_pomdp_proprio`)**: Although the wire packet carries raw kinematic headers for simulator diagnostics, all neural encoders, candidate evaluators, and policies pass the 16D somatic vector $\mathbf{p}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{16}$ through a strict POMDP filter (`strict_pomdp_observation = True`) that overwrites normalized ego coordinates $\mathbf{p}_t[0:3]$ with the agent's internal localization belief $\hat{\mathbf{x}}_t$:

$$\tilde{\mathbf{p}}_t[0:3] = \mathrm{clip}\!\left(\frac{\hat{\mathbf{x}}_t - \mathbf{w}_{\min}}{\mathbf{w}_{\max} - \mathbf{w}_{\min}},\, \mathbf{0},\, \mathbf{1}\right). \tag{1}$$

At each $60\text{ Hz}$ tick, the agent emits a hybrid motor command $a_t = (\mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{move}}, \psi_{\mathrm{aim}}, \theta_{\mathrm{aim}}, \sigma_{\mathrm{spd}}, v_{\mathrm{verb}}, g_{\mathrm{commit}}, \mathbf{g}_{\mathrm{gaze}})$, where normalized target position $\mathbf{u}_{\mathrm{move}} \in [-1, 1]^3$ and weapon orientation $(\psi_{\mathrm{aim}}, \theta_{\mathrm{aim}})$ are fully decoupled, $v_{\mathrm{verb}} \in \{0, \dots, 7\}$ selects the discrete interaction mode (approach, paint/shoot, impulse burst, evade/submerge, grasp, commit, ping, or idle/regen), $g_{\mathrm{commit}} \in \{0, 1\}$ is the basal ganglia release gate, and $\mathbf{g}_{\mathrm{gaze}}$ steers active visual scanning.

### 2.2 Two-Stage Clean-Room Arena Design (`Stage 1` & `Stage 2`)

To evaluate whole-brain integration without relying on commercial game assets or proprietary IP, we built **InkBlitz 3D** from scratch in two interoperable implementations: a full 3D simulator in Unity 6 (with procedural terrain manifolds, shaders, 48 kHz mathematically synthesized audio, and live neural HUDs) and a pure-NumPy/C++ headless competitive arena (`HeadToHeadTactical3DArena`) for deterministic verification and high-throughput ablation sweeps. Ballistics are set by the task profile: the default `turf_objective` profile uses parabolic projectiles ($v_{\mathrm{proj}} = 18.0\text{ m/s}$, $g_{\mathrm{proj}} = 11.5\text{ m/s}^2$, `ttl = 32` ticks), whereas the generic `universal_tactical` profile used in condition `C8` uses straight-line projectiles ($v_{\mathrm{proj}} = 14.0\text{ m/s}$, $g_{\mathrm{proj}} = 0$).

Because a single arena layout can confound reactive objective rushing with genuine spatial planning, both the Unity 6 simulator and the headless benchmark provide **two $180^\circ$-symmetric arena topologies** selectable via `stage_id` (`UB3D` header bits `16..31`):
- **`Stage 1: Classic Citadel` (`stage_id = 1`, Open Sightline Sprint)**: Four symmetric cover blocks $\{(\pm 4.2, 1.3, \pm 4.5)\text{ m}\}$ flank an open central lane between pedestals at $(0, 1.2, \mp 13.0)\text{ m}$ ($540$ ticks $= 9.0\text{ s}$ sprint clock). Because spawn-to-core sightlines are largely unoccluded, `Stage 1` tests fast reflexive aiming, immediate survival shielding, and whether extra spatial machinery avoids hurting short-clock direct play.
- **`Stage 2: Occluded Twin-Flank` (`stage_id = 2`, Sightline-Blocked Multi-Route Arena)**: Central sightlines between spawn and the neutral `BlitzCore` are blocked by twin staggered central baffles ($(0, 1.45, \mp 3.4)\text{ m}$ in headless scale; $(0, 2.5, \mp 5.8)\text{ m}$ in full Unity scale) and flanked by diagonal Chokepoint walls, elevated twin lateral catwalks with climbable access ramps, and symmetric mid-field flank cover ($780$ ticks $= 13.0\text{ s}$ clock, with `0.025/s` neutral decay on dropped core progress so early suicide rushes cannot coast to timeout victories). Reaching or intercepting the `BlitzCore` requires navigating around occluding geometry where unseen opponents and turf states lie outside the instantaneous camera frustum.

Across both stages, projectiles follow $\mathbf{p}_{\mathrm{proj}}(t + \Delta t) = \mathbf{p}_{\mathrm{proj}}(t) + \mathbf{v}_{\mathrm{proj}}\Delta t - \frac{1}{2}g_{\mathrm{proj}}\Delta t^2 \hat{\mathbf{y}}$ and paint floor cells upon impact. Walking on friendly turf grants dive access (`EvadeHazard` / `IdleObserve`), raising locomotion speed from $4.30\text{ m/s}$ to $6.36\text{ m/s}$ ($1.48\times$) and accelerating ink tank recovery ($0.48/\text{s}$ vs. $0.09/\text{s}$) and health regeneration ($0.28/\text{s}$ vs. $0.05/\text{s}$), subject to a $4.0\text{ s}$ dive stamina cap (`1.2 s` lockout on depletion), whereas enemy paint cuts speed to $1.55\text{ m/s}$. Seizing the central `BlitzCore` ($280\text{ HP}$ barrier in full Unity matches) reduces carrier speed ($\times 0.72$) and disables standard shooting in the symmetric 1v1 arena, shifting the carrier to route selection toward the enemy pedestal while the defender switches to interception. Depleting health triggers a mid-episode respawn (`flags |= 16`) back at base with brief invulnerability (`18` ticks), while arena turf state and dropped `BlitzCore` coordinates persist continuously until pedestal touchdown (knockout) or time limit. Floor painting rewards *only* newly flipped cells (`prev * team_sign < 0.30`, capped at 5 cells per shot), while primary dense shaping tracks signed `BlitzCore` progress delta ($\pm 8.0\,\Delta z_{\mathrm{prog}}$), combat splats, and terminal outcomes.

### 2.3 Exact $180^\circ$ Rotational Involution for Single-Policy Self-Play

In both `Stage 1` and `Stage 2`, all cover blocks, central baffles, lateral catwalks, pedestals, and opposite-sign spawn offsets $(+x_0, y_0, -z_{\mathrm{sp}}) \leftrightarrow (-x_0, y_0, +z_{\mathrm{sp}})$ ($x_0 \sim \mathcal{U}[-2, 2]$) are invariant under $180^\circ$ rotation around the arena center $\mathbf{m} = \frac{1}{2}(\mathbf{w}_{\min} + \mathbf{w}_{\max})$ (verified in Unity 6 `InkBlitzMechanicsSelfTest` Check `24/24` to `0.00` m geometric symmetry error). Simultaneous neutral objective pickups and simultaneous opposing projectile hits are resolved symmetrically without team-iteration order bias. For Team B ($\zeta = -1$, spawning North at $+Z$ and attacking $-Z$), `canonicalize_team_observation` applies an involution $\mathcal{T}_{-1}$ across pose, velocity, somatic proprioception, entity slots, and foveal horizontal normals before observations enter the brain:

$$\begin{aligned}
x' &= 2m_x - x,\quad z' = 2m_z - z,\quad (v_x', v_z') = (-v_x, -v_z),\quad (p_0', p_2', p_3', p_5') = (1 - p_0, 1 - p_2, -p_3, -p_5), \\
\psi' &= \mathrm{wrap}_\pi(\psi + \pi),\quad n_{x,\mathrm{fov}}' = -n_{x,\mathrm{fov}},
\end{aligned} \tag{2}$$

while `uncanonicalize_team_motor` applies $\tilde{\mathcal{T}}_{-1}$ to outgoing motor commands $(u_x', u_z', \psi_{\mathrm{aim}}', \psi_{\mathrm{gaze}}') \mapsto (-u_x', -u_z', \mathrm{wrap}_\pi(\psi_{\mathrm{aim}}' + \pi), \mathrm{wrap}_\pi(\psi_{\mathrm{gaze}}' + \pi))$.

> **Proposition 1 ($180^\circ$ Team-Symmetry Involution of the Observation and Motor Maps):** Let $\mathcal{T}_{-1}$ denote the observation transform in Eq. (2) and $\tilde{\mathcal{T}}_{-1}$ the corresponding motor uncanonicalization. Because $2m - (2m - q) = q$, $1 - (1 - p) = p$, $-(-v) = v$, and $\mathrm{wrap}_\pi(\mathrm{wrap}_\pi(\psi + \pi) + \pi) = \psi$ (away from the measure-zero branch cut $\psi = \pm\pi$), both continuous maps are exact involutions ($\mathcal{T}_{-1}^2 = \mathrm{Id}$, $\tilde{\mathcal{T}}_{-1}^2 = \mathrm{Id}$). If the arena's continuous kinematics were exactly $180^\circ$-symmetric, transitions would satisfy $T(\mathcal{T}_{-1}(s), a_A, a_B) = \mathcal{T}_{-1}(T(s, \tilde{\mathcal{T}}_{-1}(a_A), \tilde{\mathcal{T}}_{-1}(a_B)))$, so a single policy would face identical problems from both spawns.
>
> *Scope Remark (Implementation Asymmetry):* The implemented discrete-grid arena is not bit-identically equivariant in a single game: the zero-indexed $48 \times 48$ floor grid maps exact centerline points to one bin ($\lfloor (x - x_{\min}) / \Delta x \rfloor$), obstacle boxes and projectiles are iterated in a fixed order, and respawn offsets draw from a shared stream. We therefore never rely on single-game symmetry for evaluation. Instead, every evaluation seed is executed as a **side-swapped match pair** $((A\text{ South}, B\text{ North}), (B\text{ South}, A\text{ North}))$, canceling any fixed spawn-side offset in expectation, and we report distinct-random-seed `Full` vs. `Full` (`C0`) alongside `L2` vs. `L2` self-play to bound the empirical noise floor.
