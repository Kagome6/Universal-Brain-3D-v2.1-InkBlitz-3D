## 6. Empirical Verification & Ablation Studies

### 6.1 Experimental Protocol, Exact Nonparametric Inference, & Reward-Shaping Audit

All experiments were executed on a single consumer workstation running Windows 10 (`10.0.19045`), Python `3.10.11`, PyTorch `2.14.1+cu126`, an AMD64 16-logical-core CPU (`Family 25 Model 33`), and a single `NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060` (`12,287.5 MB` VRAM) with `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1` and deterministic seed schedules (`paper_arxiv/experiments/scripts/exp1`–`exp6`, serialized in `paper_arxiv/experiments/results/*.json`). Across all 12 trainable neural modules in `WholeBrainNeuralEngineWrapper`, the model comprises `1,053,454` trainable parameters (`rssm`: `432,323`; `slot_cortex`: `130,985`; `gwt`: `112,739`; `strat_tf`: `78,856`; `d3qn`: `65,187`; `broca`: `63,752`; `topo_adapter`: `62,197`; `foveal_enc`: `44,200`; `depth_net`: `43,165`; `self_model`: `8,577`; `concept_grounder`: `6,736`; `hazard_net`: `4,737`; plus frozen target network `d3qn_target`: `65,187`; Table 8).

All quantitative evaluations follow five methodological rules:

1. **Multi-Seed Training, Small Single-Workstation Budget, & Strict POMDP Isolation**: We trained $K = 3$ independent 4-slot, 8-generation `PBT` + `PFSP` self-play leagues (`checkpoints/remediated_v22/seed_2026`, `seed_2027`, `seed_2028`) in the headless arena with event-gated acoustics and no omniscient bearing or distance inputs, while preserving the historical baseline checkpoint (`checkpoints/user_runs/pbt_best_champion.pt`) untouched. Because both multi-seed league training and the `1,200+`-match ablation and replaceability suite were executed deterministically on a single consumer workstation without cluster or cloud GPUs, training starts from a `DAgger` bootstrap against a heuristic teacher under a deliberately compact schedule: according to the checkpoint metadata, the three champions performed `34`, `34`, and `71` gradient updates on `268`, `269`, and `537` recorded transitions. All "trained" results below should be read in that light: this early post-bootstrap regime suffices to test **systems and coupling invariants** (sub-budget $60\text{ Hz}$ latency, `74.1 MB` 8-brain VRAM scaling, zero `CRC32` errors, encoder-detached gradient orthogonality, `Plan-A` zero motor leakage, Proposition 3 $\Phi_G$ null suppression, and held-out counterfactual exposure ranking), whereas **null behavioral effects in slow or plastic pathways** cannot yet be separated from under-training. Behavioral evaluations freeze weights (`enable_learning = False`), set `policy_mode = "pure_neural"` ($0.0\%$ teacher mixing) and `strict_pomdp_observation = True` (Eq. 1), clear recurrent states between matches, and use the `seed_2026` champion unless stated otherwise.
2. **Seed-Matched Side-Swapped Pairs & Exact Conditional Sign-Flip Tests**: Each behavioral condition runs $N = 24$ independent arena seeds $\times 2$ side-swapped matches (`Full` South vs. North and vice versa; $48$ games/condition, $480$ games across `C0`–`C8` including `C6b`, plus $192$ multi-seed reproducibility games and $528$ architectural audit games in `Exp6`). Because discrete pair differences at $N \le 24$ contain many ties, `stats_utils.py` computes **exact conditional $2^{n_{\mathrm{nz}}}$ sign-flip permutation Wilcoxon signed-rank $p$-values**, exact two-sided binomial sign tests, $10{,}000$-resample bootstrap $95\%$ confidence intervals, Cohen's $d_z$, and rank-biserial correlation. Family-wise Type-I error across the six primary hypotheses (`C1`–`C6`) is controlled via Holm–Bonferroni ($p_{\mathrm{Holm}}$; [@holm1979simple]); all other $p$-values are uncorrected and exploratory.
3. **Empirical Noise Floor**: Two self-play controls estimate how far a win rate can drift from $50\%$ with no true difference: `Full` vs. `Full` with independent random salts (`C0`: `58.3%`) and the `L2` scaffold against itself in `Exp6` (`62.5%`, 95% CI `[50.0, 75.0]`). Win rates within roughly $\pm 12$ percentage points of $50\%$ that do not reach significance are therefore treated as null results.
4. **Surgical Single-Factor Ablations & Distributional Reporting**: Every ablation toggles only its target mechanism without extraneous movement-speed multipliers (`C5` sets `TIMING_MODE_FIXED_SETTLE` and `disable_bg_escape_bypass = True` while leaving candidate `speed_mod` untouched; `C6` strips only 3-head `RSSM` rollout valuation and survival shielding while keeping `gwt_workspace` and compass inputs intact; `C6b` tests pure fast `System 1` reflexes without `P5`). For latencies, $\tilde{\Phi}_G$, $\Phi_{\mathrm{act}}$, and `ignition`, we report `Mean ± SD`, `Median [IQR]`, tail percentiles, exact paired Wilcoxon or two-sided Mann–Whitney $U$ tests, and Cliff's $\delta$ [@cliff1993dominance] or Cohen's $d_z$.
5. **Separation of Shaped Return from Unshaped Victory**: Across all $432$ head-to-head games in `C1`–`C8` (`exp2_ablations.json`), the Spearman correlation between shaped-score differential and match outcome is only $\rho = 0.028$ ($p = 0.567$); in $47.1\%$ of decisive matches the agent with the higher shaped return lost. We therefore evaluate hypotheses primarily on unshaped win rate and `BlitzCore` escort progress, and report K/D, turf share, and shaped return alongside.

### 6.4 Controlled Training Dynamics: Held-Out Counterfactual Ordering & Sleep

Table 5 and Figure 3 evaluate the two learning-time mechanisms across $N = 10$ matched random seeds (`exp3_sleep_and_cf.json`, `exp5_regime_diagnostics.json`).

**Counterfactual exposure loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CF}}$, Eq. 7).** We collect natural exposed states ($\mathrm{refuge}_t < 0.5 \land \mathrm{hazard}_t \ge 0.35$, mean `27.8` states/seed) from held-out episodes not used for training and branch the simulator from each state (`copy.deepcopy(arena)`) under `M4: EvadeRefuge` and `M7: IdleObserve`. In these ground-truth rollouts, escaping survives three steps with probability **`0.624 ± 0.097`** and idling with **`0.349 ± 0.059`**. A model trained without Eq. (7) (`CF Loss OFF`) inverts this ordering, predicting `0.270 ± 0.094` for escape and `0.337 ± 0.081` for idling (gap `-0.067 ± 0.057`, 95% CI `[-0.101, -0.034]`). With Eq. (7) (`CF Loss ON`) the ordering is correct in **all $10/10$ seeds** ($\hat{S}_{M_4} = 0.244 \pm 0.063$ vs. $\hat{S}_{M_7} = 0.123 \pm 0.021$; paired gap shift `+0.188`, exact Wilcoxon $p = 0.0020$, $d_z = +5.22$). Two caveats apply. First, Eq. (7) explicitly pushes $\hat{S}_{M_7}$ toward `0.12` on exposed states, so the corrected ordering is partly built into the training target; the held-out test shows that this target transfers to unseen states, not that the model discovered the ordering independently. Second, absolute predictions are poorly calibrated under both settings (true values `0.624`/`0.349`), and Eq. (7) makes the `M7` Brier score worse (`0.151 ± 0.052` vs. `0.117 ± 0.042`, $p = 0.0020$).

**Offline sleep (`S-1..S-4`).** Sleep did not produce a detectable benefit. Q-values stayed bounded ($|Q|_{\max} = 0.666 \pm 0.162$ with sleep vs. `0.521 ± 0.138` without, far below the `50.0` rollback ceiling), win rate against the no-sleep variant was at chance both in short single-agent runs (`Exp3A`: `52.5% [37.5, 65.0]%`, $p = 1.000$) and over larger buffers (`Exp5`: $N_{\mathrm{buf}} = 651.1$ transitions, `72` draws, replay-to-buffer ratio `0.111`, [@fedus2020revisiting]; win rate `45.0% [30.0, 62.5]%`, $p = 0.781$), and held-out TD error (`0.192 ± 0.041` vs. `0.207 ± 0.103`, $p = 0.922$) and visual MAE (`0.2103` vs. `0.2102`) did not differ. In the PBT leagues, consolidation loss fell from the first to the last generation in all three seeds (`6.26 -> 5.25`, `6.52 -> 5.37`, `6.36 -> 5.27`), but not monotonically, and this trend cannot be separated from ordinary training progress.

### 6.5 Pillar C: Integration Diagnostics ($\Phi_G$, $\Phi_{\mathrm{act}}$), Workspace Habituation, & `Plan-A`

We examine internal dynamics across $N = 50$ synthetic Monte Carlo trials ($T = 48$) and $N = 24$ independent live episodes (`exp4_consciousness_wm.json`; Table 6, Figures 4–5).

1. **Proposition 3 Monte Carlo (`Figure 5a`)**: Under shared sinusoidal + Gaussian common input with zero cross-module causal weights ($\mathbf{A}_{\mathrm{true}} = \mathrm{diag}(a_i)$), unregularized OLS reports `0.274 ± 0.151 bits`, GCV isotropic ridge `0.061 ± 0.051 bits`, and penalty-matched $\lambda = 0.14$ isotropic ridge `0.051 ± 0.054 bits`. Off-diagonal ridge with debiasing (Eqs. 13, 15) reports **`0.000 bits` in all $50/50$ common-input trials** (`0.028 ± 0.018 bits` before debiasing) and in all $50/50$ independent AR(1) ($\rho = 0.9$) trials. On coupled `2+3` block systems, Eq. (15) recovers the true partition in **`70.0%` (`35/50`)** of windows (chance $1/15 = 6.7\%$), reporting `0.070 ± 0.058 bits` (`0.088 ± 0.071 bits` at the true cut, consistent with the shrinkage noted in Proposition 3), and `0.276 ± 0.118 bits` on 5-node directed rings.
2. **Live $\tilde{\Phi}_G$ and $\Phi_{\mathrm{act}}$ (`Figures 4 & 5c`)**: Live $\tilde{\Phi}_G$ values are very small (trained median `8.6e-5 bits`) and sit close to the debiasing floor. They exceed time-shuffled surrogates (`0.51 ± 0.86` vs. `0.01 ± 0.05 × 10^-3 bits`, `17-0-7`, exact $p = 1.5 \times 10^{-5}$) but do not differ between trained and untrained networks (`0.89 ± 1.69 × 10^-3 bits`, paired $p = 0.585$). Within episodes, $\tilde{\Phi}_G$ is lower at crisis/veto steps than at cruise steps (`0.74 ± 1.42` $\to$ `0.16 ± 0.41 × 10^-3 bits`, exact $p = 0.0046$), whereas policy-override divergence $\Phi_{\mathrm{act}}$ is higher in `24/24` episodes (`0.0910 ± 0.0116` $\to$ `0.1311 ± 0.0218 bits`, $p = 1.2 \times 10^{-7}$, $d_z = +1.48$), as is `self_surprise` (`0.1072` $\to$ `0.1146`, $p = 1.2 \times 10^{-7}$). Because crisis steps are defined by the shield overriding the reflex policy, the $\Phi_{\mathrm{act}}$ increase is largely expected by construction; we report it as a consistency check of the instrumentation rather than as an independent finding. Given the magnitudes involved, we do not interpret the $\tilde{\Phi}_G$ decrease mechanistically.
3. **Workspace habituation (`Figure 5b`)**: Removing the full novelty-plus-occupancy habituation circuit (`Full Hab-OFF`) reduces specialist handoffs tenfold (`7.64 ± 0.72` $\to$ `0.72 ± 0.62 /s`, $p = 1.2 \times 10^{-7}$, $d_z = +8.04$), lowers ignition (`0.250` $\to$ `0.205`), and *raises* normalized broadcast entropy (`0.939` $\to$ `0.978`). Without habituation, the workspace thus settles into a nearly uniform, rarely changing broadcast rather than a single-specialist monopoly. Removing only the occupancy fatigue trace while keeping the novelty term (`Surgical Hab-OFF`) has a much smaller effect (`7.64` $\to$ `7.34 /s`, `17-4-3`, $p = 0.0036$, $d_z = +0.69$), so most of the handoff dynamics come from the novelty-driven term.
4. **World-model accuracy & `Plan-A` isolation**: Trained weights reduce next-step `16x16` visual prediction MAE slightly but significantly (`0.2163 ± 0.0073` $\to$ `0.2106 ± 0.0055`, `19-5`, $p = 0.0014$, $d_z = +0.65$); depth MAE does not change significantly (`5.84 ± 0.19 m` $\to$ `5.80 ± 0.13 m`, $p = 0.277$). Across `500` live states, toggling the Broca bias under `Plan-A` changes no candidate Q-value (`max|ΔQ| = 0.000000`); as noted in Section 4.4, the RVQ symbol prior is not covered by this check.

### 6.6 Pillar D: Three-Criteria Architectural Integration Audit

Modules that coexist in software do not necessarily function as one system: they may break when swapped, cancel each other's gradients on shared trunks, ride along without influencing behavior, or hide what degrades when a new organ is added. Table 7 and Figure 6 (`exp6_architecture_criteria.json`) evaluate Universal Brain 3D (v2.2) against three criteria.

1. **Criterion 1 — Replaceability (`S1`–`S5`, `240` matches, `0` contract errors)**: Replacing `TT-D3QN` with zero-shot `RSSM` model-predictive control (`S1`), replacing workspace competition with uniform $1/5$ pooling (`S2`) or hard one-hot winner-take-all (`S3`), replacing loop closure with dead reckoning (`S4`), and replacing adaptive `STN-SNr` gating with `1.35x` braking (`S5`) all ran `48` matches each without `NaN`, shape, or protocol violations. Behaviorally, only `S1` matters: removing `TT-D3QN` drops win rate against the `L2` scaffold to `25.0%` (`2-14-8`, exact $p = 0.0042$, $d_z = -0.76$; stagnation `0.281 -> 0.456`). `S2`–`S5` stay at `49`–`54%`. This shows that these components can be swapped without breaking the system, but it also shows that we could not detect a behavioral effect of the workspace competition rule, loop closure, or basal-ganglia gain at this training budget; the two interpretations cannot be separated with these data.
2. **Criterion 2 — Gradient interference and link sensitivity**: We computed per-loss parameter gradients for all **9 training objectives** (`L_td, L_speed, L_rssm, L_cf, L_vis, L_slot, L_strat, L_self, L_lang`) on shared replay batches. The audit script reproduces the engine's loss terms rather than hooking the production training step, so it verifies the loss design, not bit-level identity with the engine. Across the $9 \times 9$ matrix of cosines restricted to shared parameters, the minimum is `-0.0227` (`L_rssm` vs. `L_slot`); `cos(L_rssm, L_cf) = +0.272` on `rssm`, and `cos(L_td, L_vis) = +0.008` on `foveal_enc`, where the visual gradient norm (`0.0161`) now exceeds the TD gradient norm (`0.0040`). The absence of overlap between `L_cf` and the encoders follows directly from the stop-gradients in Eq. (7) and is therefore a design property rather than an empirical finding. Mean-clamping each of 10 cross-module forward links over `186` live states identifies the load-bearing pathways—the `24D` memory compass (`L5`: `32.8%` action flips, `KL = 0.125 bits`), 3-head `RSSM` imagination (`L7`: `16.1%`), `MEC/HD` codes (`L4`: `2.7%`), `SlotCortex` (`L2`: `1.6%`), and workspace broadcast (`L8`: `1.1%`)—and two pathways that are effectively silent at this budget: the zero-initialized $t-1 \to t$ slot prior (`L3`, `0.0%`) and `StrategicTransformer` input to the workspace (`L9`, `0.0%`). The self-model/RVQ link (`L10`) also produced no action flips (`max|dQ| = 0.0095`).
3. **Criterion 3 — Six-rung cumulative organ ladder (`L0 -> L5`, each against `L2`)**: Measuring win rate, reach, kills, deaths, turf, shots, and stagnation together makes the trade-offs explicit (`Table 7`, `Figure 6a`). The `L2` row is `L2` playing itself and should be read as a self-play control; its `62.5%` illustrates the noise floor. Adding Kalman localization and the `48x48` map (`L0 -> L1`) raises turf share from `15.5%` to `19.6%` but not win rate. Replacing the hand-crafted selector with the trained `TT-D3QN` (`L3`) raises kills (`0.65 -> 0.79`) but **loses to the scaffold** (`35.4%`, $p = 0.039$). Adding the `RSSM` and shield (`L4`) raises kills further (`0.88`) and lowers deaths (`0.81 -> 0.73`) while increasing stagnation (`0.341`) and losing to the scaffold (`31.3%`, $p = 0.022$). The full system (`L5`), which adds target-gated basal ganglia, workspace, and self-model at once, reduces stagnation (`0.341 -> 0.281`) and reaches **parity with the hand-crafted scaffold (`50.0%`, `7-7-10`)**, with the highest reach (`58.2%`) and kills (`0.90`) on the ladder. Because three components are added at once, this step does not isolate the contribution of the basal-ganglia fix, and shots per game remain lower than on rungs `L0`–`L3` (`18.9` vs. `20.9`–`22.0`). In short, at this training budget the learned components do not yet outperform the non-neural scaffold they are built on.
