## 6. Empirical Verification & Ablation Studies

### 6.1 Experimental Protocol, Exact Nonparametric Inference, & Reward-Shaping Audit

All experiments were executed on a single consumer workstation running Windows 10 (`10.0.19045`), Python `3.10.11`, PyTorch `2.14.1+cu126`, an AMD64 16-logical-core CPU (`Family 25 Model 33`), and a single `NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060` (`12,287.5 MB` VRAM) with `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1`, native Zero-PyTorch C++ `/O2` spatial kernels (`fast_brain3d_kernels.dll`), and deterministic seed schedules (`paper_v2_3/experiments/scripts/` and `scripts/run_v23_phase_d_experiments.py`, serialized in `paper_v2_3/experiments/results/*.json`). Across all 12 trainable neural modules in `WholeBrainNeuralEngineWrapper`, Universal Brain 3D (v2.3.0) comprises `1,054,099` trainable parameters (`rssm`: `432,323`; `slot_cortex`: `130,985`; `gwt`: `113,384`; `strat_tf`: `78,856`; `d3qn`: `65,187`; `broca`: `63,752`; `topo_adapter`: `62,197`; `foveal_enc`: `44,200`; `depth_net`: `43,165`; `self_model`: `8,577`; `concept_grounder`: `6,736`; `hazard_net`: `4,737`; plus EMA target network `d3qn_target`: `65,187`; Table 10).

All quantitative evaluations follow five methodological rules:

1. **Multi-Seed Progressive `Scaffold Annealing` Training & Autonomous `scaf = 0.0` Evaluation**: We trained $K = 3$ independent self-play runs (`checkpoints/v23_full_study/seed_2026`, `seed_2027`, `seed_2028`) across `10` generations of mixed `Stage 1` (`Classic Citadel`) and `Stage 2` (`Occluded Twin-Flank`) arenas under strict POMDP observation (`strict_pomdp_observation = True`, Eq. 1) with event-gated acoustics and zero omniscient bearing leaks. Using an `8`-worker Zero-PyTorch CPU arena pool multiplexed onto one `PopGPUServer` (`75.6–75.9 MB` peak VRAM), the three seeds completed `3,440`, `3,472`, and `3,467` gradient updates (`10,379` total updates; `6,550`–`6,614` transitions and `10` 4-phase Sleep `S-1..S-4` cycles per seed) with **`0` `NaN`/emergency rollbacks**. Crucially, training annealed `DAgger` imitation mixing to `0.0` by Generation 3 (`0.55 -> 0.18 -> 0.00`) and progressively annealed the non-neural topological compass prior $\lambda_{\mathrm{scaf}}$ (`scaffold_prior_scale`, Eq. 8) along `1.00 -> 0.80 -> 0.55 -> 0.35 -> 0.15 -> 0.00` over Generations 1–6, holding $\lambda_{\mathrm{scaf}} = 0.0$ through Generation 10. All primary behavioral evaluations freeze weights (`enable_learning = False`), set `policy_mode = "pure_neural"` ($0.0\%$ teacher mixing) and **`scaffold_prior_scale = 0.0` (100% Autonomous Neural Mode)**, clear recurrent states between matches, and select best checkpoints via autonomous validation win rate (`seed_2026` serves as the fixed single-checkpoint target in `Exp1`, `Exp2`, `Exp4`, and `Exp6`, complemented by pooled `K = 3` multi-seed tests).
2. **Seed-Matched Side-Swapped Pairs & Exact Conditional Sign-Flip Tests**: Each behavioral condition runs $N = 24$ independent arena seeds $\times 2$ side-swapped matches (`Full` South vs. North and vice versa; $48$ games/condition across `11` conditions in `Exp2` $= 528$ games, `144` games/row in pooled `K = 3` multi-seed cross-stage tests $= 1,008$ games, and `528` audit games across `S1`–`S5` and `L0`–`L5` in `Exp6`). Because discrete pair differences at $N = 24$ contain ties, `stats_utils.py` computes **exact conditional $2^{n_{\mathrm{nz}}}$ sign-flip permutation Wilcoxon signed-rank $p$-values**, exact two-sided binomial sign tests, $10{,}000$-resample bootstrap $95\%$ confidence intervals, Cohen's $d_z$, and rank-biserial correlation. Family-wise Type-I error across the ten non-control hypotheses in Table 5 is controlled via step-down Holm–Bonferroni ($p_{\mathrm{Holm}}$; [@holm1979simple]).
3. **Empirical Noise Floor**: Two symmetric self-play controls bound finite-sample drift from $50\%$: autonomous `Full` vs. `Full` with distinct random salts (`C0`: `54.2%`, 95% CI `[39.6, 68.8]%`, $p = 0.774$) and the `L2` heuristic scaffold against itself on `Stage 2` (`47.9%`, 95% CI `[33.3, 64.6]%`, $p = 1.000$). Single-seed differences within roughly $\pm 12$ percentage points of $50\%$ that do not reach statistical significance are treated as null results.
4. **Surgical Single-Factor Ablations & Distributional Reporting**: Every ablation toggles only its target mechanism without extraneous movement-speed multipliers (`C5` sets `TIMING_MODE_FIXED_SETTLE` and `disable_bg_escape_bypass = True` while leaving candidate `speed_mod` untouched; `C6` strips only 3-head `RSSM` rollout valuation and survival shielding; `C6b` tests pure fast `System 1` `q_raw_values` reflexes; `C7` zeros only the 128D `gwt_workspace` vector entering `TT-D3QN` and arbitration).
5. **Separation of Shaped Return from Unshaped Victory**: Across all $480$ autonomous head-to-head games in `C1`–`C8` (`exp2_ablations.json`), shaped-score differential correlates positively with unshaped victory ($\rho = 0.524$, $p = 3.4 \times 10^{-35}$, `74.3%` decisive sign agreement), yet in **`25.7%` of decisive matches the agent with higher shaped return still lost** (most notably when paint/combat points trade off against raw `BlitzCore` distance at timeout). Unshaped match win rate and `BlitzCore` escort progress therefore remain our primary evaluation metrics.

### 6.4 Controlled Training Dynamics: Held-Out Counterfactual Ordering & Sleep

Table 7 and Figure 3 evaluate the two learning-time mechanisms across $N = 10$ matched random seeds (`exp3_sleep_and_cf.json`, `exp5_regime_diagnostics.json`).

**Counterfactual exposure loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CF}}$, Eq. 7).** On natural exposed states ($\mathrm{refuge}_t < 0.5 \land \mathrm{hazard}_t \ge 0.35$, mean `27.8` states/seed) from unseen held-out episodes, ground-truth interventional simulator rollouts survive three steps with probability **`0.624 ± 0.097`** under `M4: EvadeRefuge` versus **`0.349 ± 0.059`** under `M7: IdleObserve`. Training without Eq. (7) (`CF Loss OFF`) inverts this ranking (`0.270 ± 0.094` escape vs. `0.337 ± 0.081` idle; gap `-0.067 ± 0.057`). Enabling Eq. (7) (`CF Loss ON`) restores the positive escape-over-idle survival ordering in **all $10/10$ seeds** ($\hat{S}_{M_4} = 0.244 \pm 0.063$ vs. $\hat{S}_{M_7} = 0.123 \pm 0.021$; paired gap shift `+0.188`, exact Wilcoxon $p = 0.0020$, $d_z = +5.22$). As an explicit caveat, Eq. (7) supervises $\hat{S}_{M_7}$ toward a conservative `0.12` target on exposed states, which transfers the correct ordinal ranking to unseen states at the price of under-predicting absolute survival probability and worsening the exposed `M7` Brier score (`0.151 ± 0.052` vs. `0.117 ± 0.042`, $p = 0.0020$).

**Offline sleep (`S-1..S-4`).** Across controlled single-agent comparisons, 4-phase sleep keeps Q-values bounded ($|Q|_{\max} = 0.666 \pm 0.162$, well below the `50.0` rollback ceiling) and reduces generational consolidation loss across all three `v2.3.0` self-play seeds (`5.34 -> 4.40`, `5.13 -> 4.49`, `5.15 -> 4.45`), yet produces **no statistically detectable head-to-head win-rate advantage** over online-only learning in either micro-buffer (`Exp3A`: `52.5% [37.5, 65.0]%`, $p = 1.000$) or diverse-buffer regimes (`Exp5`: $N_{\mathrm{buf}} = 651.1$, replay ratio `0.111`, [@fedus2020revisiting]; win rate `45.0% [30.0, 62.5]%`, $p = 0.781$; held-out TD MAE `0.192 ± 0.041` vs. `0.207 ± 0.103`, $p = 0.922$).

### 6.5 Pillar C: Integration Diagnostics ($\Phi_G$, $\Phi_{\mathrm{act}}$), Workspace Habituation, & `UBWM` Accuracy

We examine internal dynamics across $N = 50$ synthetic Monte Carlo trials ($T = 48$) and $N = 24$ independent live episodes on the trained `v2.3.0` champion (`exp4_consciousness_wm.json`; Table 8, Figures 4–5).

1. **Proposition 3 Monte Carlo (`Figure 5a`)**: Under shared common-input flashes with zero cross-module causal links ($\mathbf{A}_{\mathrm{true}} = \mathrm{diag}(a_i)$), unregularized OLS reports `0.274 ± 0.151 bits`, GCV isotropic ridge `0.061 ± 0.051 bits`, and penalty-matched $\lambda = 0.14$ isotropic ridge `0.051 ± 0.054 bits`. Off-diagonal ridge with debiasing (Eqs. 14, 16) reports **`0.000 bits` in all $50/50$ common-input trials** and all $50/50$ AR(1) ($\rho = 0.9$) trials, while recovering the exact `2+3` block partition in **`70.0%` (`35/50`)** of windows (`0.070 ± 0.058 bits`; chance $6.7\%$) and reporting `0.276 ± 0.118 bits` on 5-node directed rings.
2. **Trained vs. Untrained Lagged Causal Integration $\tilde{\Phi}_G$ (`Figures 4 & 5c`)**: Unlike under-trained checkpoints where live $\tilde{\Phi}_G$ stays indistinguishable from random initialization, the `3,440`-update `v2.3.0` champion exhibits **`9.38 ± 6.63 × 10^-3 bits` (`median = 8.67 × 10^-3 bits`)** across live matches—exceeding both **untrained weights (`0.04 ± 0.11 × 10^-3 bits`, paired exact Wilcoxon $p = 2.4 \times 10^{-7}$)** and **time-shuffled surrogates (`0.39 ± 0.52 × 10^-3 bits`, `23-1-0`, $p = 2.4 \times 10^{-7}$)** by more than an order of magnitude. Within autonomous episodes ($\lambda_{\mathrm{scaf}} = 0.0$), neither $\tilde{\Phi}_G$ (`12.62` vs. `8.28 × 10^-3 bits`, $p = 0.277$) nor $\Phi_{\mathrm{act}}$ (`0.0754` vs. `0.0728 bits`, $p = 0.491$) shifts significantly between cruise and crisis steps—reflecting that `System 1` (`TT-D3QN`) has largely aligned with `System 2` survival preferences during training—whereas metacognitive `self_surprise` $u_{\mathrm{self}}$ rises significantly under crisis (`0.0601 ± 0.0171` $\to$ `0.0691 ± 0.0122`, $p = 0.049$, $d_z = +0.44$).
3. **Workspace Habituation Prevents Trained Specialist Monopoly (`Figure 5b`)**: Once specialists are trained for `3,440` updates, disabling the habituation circuit (`Full Hab-OFF`) causes **genuine single-specialist lock-in**: ignition saturates at `0.974`, normalized specialist entropy drops from `0.512` to `0.352`, and handoffs fall threefold from **`4.17 ± 0.66 /s` to `1.39 ± 0.58 /s` ($p = 1.2 \times 10^{-7}$, $d_z = +3.89$)**. Crucially, surgically zeroing *only* the occupancy fatigue trace $\bar{\alpha}_k$ while keeping phasic novelty intact (`Surgical Hab-OFF`) also drops handoffs sharply to **`2.48 /s` ($p = 1.2 \times 10^{-7}$, $d_z = +3.40$)**, demonstrating that both broadcast-occupancy fatigue and novelty salience in Eq. (10) do substantial work in trained workspaces.
4. **Substantial World-Model `UBWM` Gains & Exact `Plan-A` Isolation**: Because world-model encoder ownership (`Sec. 4.2`) shields `foveal_enc` and `slot_cortex` from TD gradient interference, next-step `16x16` visual prediction MAE drops by more than half from **`0.2138 ± 0.0057` (`Untrained`) to `0.0934 ± 0.0089` ($p = 1.2 \times 10^{-7}$, $d_z = +11.07$)**, and **3D depth prediction MAE drops fourfold from `5.88 ± 0.16 m` to `1.45 ± 0.23 m` ($p = 1.2 \times 10^{-7}$)**. Across `500` live states, toggling Broca motor bias under `Plan-A` yields `max|ΔQ| = 0.000000` (Eq. 12).

1. **Criterion 1 — Replaceability (`S1`–`S5`, `240` matches, `0` contract errors)**: All five cross-layer swaps ran `48` matches on occluded `Stage 2` with **`0` `NaN`, shape, or protocol errors**. Removing `TT-D3QN` in favor of zero-shot `RSSM` MPC (`S1`) drops win rate by `-14.6` points (`79.2% -> 64.6%`, deaths halved to `0.38`, stagnation rising `0.233 -> 0.291`), and removing Kalman loop closure (`S4`) drops win rate by **`-10.4` points (`79.2% -> 68.8%`, reach `67.2% -> 62.4%`)**, indicating measurable contributions from both `TT-D3QN` and loop-closed localization on occluded terrain.
