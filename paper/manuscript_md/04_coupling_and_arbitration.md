## 4. Cross-Module Coupling & Arbitration Principles

Rather than re-deriving standard deep learning layers, this section specifies the mathematical interfaces, invariance filters, gradient-isolation boundaries, and arbitration equations that resolve Pathologies P2–P5 when coupling spatial memory, subcortical control, world-model imagination, global workspace broadcasting, and language.

### 4.1 Spatial Memory, Respawn Continuity, & Decoupled 6-DoF Ballistic Control (Resolving P2)

#### 3D Entorhinal Coding and Stationary-Landmark Scalar Kalman Localization
From believed 3D position $\hat{\mathbf{x}}_t \in \mathbb{R}^3$ and gaze $(\psi_t, \theta_t)$, `EntorhinalHippocampal3DEngineV2` computes a 256D multi-scale grid code $\mathbf{g}_{\mathrm{MEC}} \in \mathbb{R}^{256}$ projected onto a 3D Face-Centered-Cubic (FCC) lattice basis $\mathbf{B}_{\mathrm{FCC}} \in \mathbb{R}^{3\times 3}$ across four wavelengths $\lambda_s \in \{1.6, 3.4, 7.2, 15.0\}\text{ m}$ and $4^3 = 64$ phase offsets $\boldsymbol{\phi}_j \in [0, 2\pi)^3$ [@hafting2005grid; @banino2018vector], alongside a 32D Fibonacci-sphere head-direction code $h_k = \exp(3.5(\mathbf{u}_k^\top \mathbf{d}_{\mathrm{gaze}} - 1))$.

Between landmark sightings, dead-reckoning integrates velocity under bounded Gaussian drift $\hat{\mathbf{x}}_t^- = \mathrm{clip}(\hat{\mathbf{x}}_{t-1} + \mathbf{v}_t \Delta t + \boldsymbol{\eta}_t, \mathbf{w}_{\min}, \mathbf{w}_{\max})$ with $\sigma_{\mathrm{odom}} = 0.035\,s_{\mathrm{odom}}\sqrt{\Delta t}$ and scalar isotropic position-variance growth $P_t^- = \min(2.5, P_{t-1} + 0.08\Delta t)$. To prevent Pathology P2 (moving agents or carried objectives dragging self-localization), scalar-gain Kalman loop closure [@durrantwhyte2006slam] is gated by a strict semantic-kinematic predicate $\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{static}}(\mathrm{tag}_i, \|\mathbf{v}_i\|)$ that admits *only* fixed geometry and pedestals:

$$K_i = \frac{P_t^-}{P_t^- + 0.18 / \max(0.2, c_i)}, \quad \hat{\mathbf{x}}_t \leftarrow \hat{\mathbf{x}}_t^- + K_i(\mathbf{a}_i - \mathbf{z}_i), \quad P_t \leftarrow \max\!\left(0.02, (1 - K_i)P_t^-\right), \tag{4}$$

applied only when innovation $\|\mathbf{a}_i - \mathbf{z}_i\|_2 < 3.5\text{ m}$ against stored landmark anchor $\mathbf{a}_i$ (updated via slow $0.04$ exponential averaging rather than a joint $3N$-dimensional covariance matrix, which keeps the per-tick update to a few scalar operations per landmark). Dynamic entities bypass Eq. (4) and enter a multi-timescale object working memory whose confidence decays exponentially while unseen, accelerated to $\lambda_{\mathrm{dis}} = 5.5\text{ s}^{-1}$ if a 1st-person foveal ray passes unobstructed *beyond* a remembered opponent's expected coordinates (1st-person visual disconfirmation). When unseen directional audio exceeds $\ell_{\mathrm{aud}} \ge 0.18$, an acoustic phantom threat slot (`id = 9991`) is synthesized along the estimated bearing so audible flankers remain representable in downstream planning.

#### Four-Layer Allocentric Field Map and Mid-Episode Respawn (`flags |= 16`)
`MultiLayerCognitiveFieldMap3D` discretizes the horizontal arena into $48 \times 48$ cells ($0.50\text{ m} \times 0.67\text{ m}$ per cell in standard bounds) storing four layers integrated at 60 Hz from unprojected $16 \times 16$ foveal rays and footstep proprioception. The first layer tracks occupancy (`occ_state` $\in \{\text{UNKNOWN}, \text{FREE}, \text{WALL}\}$) from accumulated vertical normal evidence ($|n_y| < 0.42$). The second layer stores epistemically decaying surface valence $S_t(z, x) \in [-1, +1]$: because opponents can paint over unobserved turf at any time, cells unobserved for $\tau_{\mathrm{age}} > 1.0\text{ s}$ decay exponentially toward epistemic neutrality $0.0$ at rate $\lambda_{\mathrm{surf}} = 0.060\text{ s}^{-1}$:

$$S_t(z, x) \leftarrow S_{t-1}(z, x) \cdot \exp(-\lambda_{\mathrm{surf}} \Delta t) \quad \text{for } \tau_{\mathrm{age}}(z, x) > 1.0\text{ s}, \quad \lambda_{\mathrm{surf}} = 0.060\text{ s}^{-1}. \tag{5}$$

The third layer tracks relative 3D elevation (`elev_map` $\in [-4, +4]\text{ m}$), preventing path planning across unclimbable ledges ($\Delta h > 0.62\text{ m}$), while the fourth layer stores `visit_freshness` $\in [0, 1]$ and `strategic_hazard_scar`, which deposits a localized repulsion potential ($+0.95 / (1 + 0.45 d^2)$) at elimination coordinates. Upon mid-episode respawn (`flags |= 16`), the brain executes an **asymmetric state split**: $\hat{\mathbf{x}}_t$ re-anchors to spawn with $P_t \leftarrow 0.04$, transient hostile memory slots clear, and `surface_map` ages modestly ($\times 0.85$) to reflect respawn downtime—while **preserving `occ_state`, `elev_map`, landmark anchors, and death-site hazard scars**. Across full episode boundaries on the same `stage_id`, `stage_schema_bank` carries over *only* structural geometry (`occ_state`, `wall_score`, `elev_map`) while zeroing 100% of `surface_map` so prior-match paint is never hallucinated.

#### Cerebellar 3D Ballistic Lead-Aim and the 8-Candidate 6-DoF Generator
`Cerebellar3DKinematicPredictorV2` computes projectile interception independently of body motion via two fixed-point iterations $t_{\mathrm{hit}}^{(k+1)} = \|\mathbf{p}_{\mathrm{tgt}} + \mathbf{v}_{\mathrm{tgt}} t_{\mathrm{hit}}^{(k)} - \hat{\mathbf{x}}_t\|_2 / v_{\mathrm{proj}}$ clamped to $[0, 1.15]\text{ s}$, adding exact parabolic gravity drop compensation $\Delta y = \min(1.05, \frac{1}{2} g_{\mathrm{proj}} t_{\mathrm{hit}}^2)$ matched to simulator projectile gravity $g_{\mathrm{proj}}$. Using a surface-and-threat-aware A* planner on the `48x48` map (`step_cost` penalized by $+1.15 S^-$, $+1.35\,\text{scar}$, and enemy line-of-fire corridors), `propose_universal_3d_candidates` constructs the 24D memory compass $\mathbf{c}_t$ and the 8 decoupled candidates `M0..M7` (`Objective Advance`, `Frontier/Turf Paint`, `Strafe-Right Lead-Engage`, `Wing/Flank Detour`, `Evade/Refuge Dive`, `Shield-Break/Burst`, `Pickup/Defend Rally`, `Idle/Submerge Regen`), each featurized into a 32D candidate-compass alignment vector $\boldsymbol{\phi}_m \in \mathbb{R}^{32}$.

### 4.2 Three-Way Arbitration, Gradient-Isolated Counterfactuals, & Escape Exemption (Resolving P3)

#### Multi-Step 3-Head Categorical RSSM with Encoder-Detached Counterfactual De-Confounding
`CategoricalRSSM3D` maintains a deterministic recurrent state $\mathbf{h}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{256}$ (`GRUCell`) and an $8 \times 8$ discrete stochastic state $\mathbf{s}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{64}$ parameterized with $1\%$ uniform unimix straight-through gradients [@hafner2023dreamerv3]. Given posterior $(\mathbf{h}_t, \mathbf{s}_t)$, the world model executes a parallel open-loop imagination rollout of horizon $H = 3$ cognitive cycles ($\approx 250\text{ ms}$ at $12\text{ Hz}$, matching projectile flight duration and the $260\text{ ms}$ exposed basal ganglia gate ceiling) across all $M = 8$ candidate features $\boldsymbol{\phi}_m$, predicting step rewards $r_{m,k}$, step-wise survival indicators $s_{m,k} \in (0, 1)$, and territorial/objective advance probabilities $a_{m,k} \in (0, 1)$. Multi-step survival uses a conservative **tail-weighted risk heuristic** rather than an independent product so an acute threat at step 1 or 2 lowers the composite survival score without collapsing multiplicative scale:

$$\hat{R}_m = 0.70 r_{m,0} + 0.20 r_{m,1} + 0.10 r_{m,2}, \qquad \hat{S}_m = 0.75 s_{m,0} + 0.25 \min(s_{m,1}, s_{m,2}). \tag{6}$$

To resolve Pathology P3—where `M7: IdleObserve` is executed predominantly inside safe cover during behavioral play and therefore learns spuriously high observational survival predictions in open crossfire—online and offline updates apply an explicit **counterfactual open-space exposure loss** $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CF}}$ on transitions outside refuge under active hazard ($\mathrm{refuge}_t < 0.5 \land \mathrm{hazard}_t \ge 0.35$). However, backpropagating synthetic counterfactual targets directly into the shared visual encoder (`foveal_enc`) and factual `RSSM` posterior corrupts perceptual depth/hazard features and factual transition dynamics ($\cos(\nabla\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CF}}, \nabla\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{vis}}) = -0.733$ and $\cos(\nabla\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CF}}, \nabla\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{rssm}}) = -0.457$ prior to isolation; [@yu2020pcgrad]). We therefore apply stop-gradient boundaries $\mathrm{sg}[\cdot]$ on the factual latent states $(\mathbf{h}_t, \mathbf{s}_t, \mathbf{z}_{\mathrm{eff}}, \mathbf{w}_t)$ so $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CF}}$ shapes *only* the counterfactual action-transition rollout and candidate value heads, paired with $0.25$ straight-through gradient scaling (`0.25 z_perc + 0.75 sg[z_perc]`) on the `TT-D3QN` visual trunk so TD updates cannot overwhelm self-supervised depth/hazard learning:

$$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{CF}} = \mathbb{I}_{\mathrm{exposed}} \cdot \left[ 0.45\,\mathrm{BCE}\!\left(\hat{S}_{M_7}(\mathrm{sg}[\mathbf{h}_t], \mathrm{sg}[\mathbf{s}_t]), 0.12\right) + 0.25\,\mathrm{ReLU}\!\left(Q(\mathrm{sg}[\mathbf{z}_{\mathrm{eff}}, \mathbf{w}_t, \mathbf{h}_t], M_7) - \mathrm{sg}[Q(s_t, m_t)] + 0.60\right) \right]. \tag{7}$$

Equation (7) teaches both the imagination rollout and `D3QN` action-feature head that standing idle in open crossfire is hazardous while leaving shared visual and posterior representations untouched by this loss (audited in Section 6.6 with a reproduction of the training losses).

#### Asymmetric Survival Shield and Target-Gated Basal Ganglia Gate
At $12\text{ Hz}$, `ConsciousGWTProcess` first fuses `TensorTrainD3QNCandidateNet3D` values (`TTLinear` rank-16 trunk `768 -> 192`; [@novikov2015tt]; plus dueling heads) with world-model rollouts and the RVQ proto-symbol prior, $Q_{\mathrm{fused}}(m) = Q_{\mathrm{D3QN}}(m) + 0.30\,\hat{R}_m + 0.25(\hat{A}_m - 0.5) + 0.15\,b_{\mathrm{proto}}(m)$ (plus a Broca term that is identically zero under `Plan-A`, Eq. 11). It then applies topological drives, action-stagnation habituation, and—when `enable_safety_shield = True` [@alshiekh2018safe]—two asymmetric safety vetoes:

$$Q^\star(m) = Q_{\mathrm{fused}}(m) + I(m) - 0.42\,h_{\mathrm{act}}(m) - G(m) - \begin{cases} 2.85 & \text{if exposed}_{\mathrm{S2}} \land v_m = \text{Idle} \\ 4.5\,\mathrm{ReLU}(\theta_{\mathrm{surv}} - \hat{S}_m)\,\mathbb{I}[\neg \mathrm{esc}_m] & \text{otherwise}, \end{cases} \tag{8}$$

where $\text{exposed}_{\mathrm{S2}}$ holds outside refuge when $\mathrm{hazard}_t \ge 0.55$ (or $\ge 0.32$ with ink tank $< 0.16$), and $\mathrm{esc}_m$ is the **Active Escape Exemption** predicate (`verb == EvadeHazard`, moving away from threat $\mathbf{u}_m^\top \mathbf{d}_{\mathrm{thr}} < -0.15$, or moving into cover $\mathbf{u}_m^\top \mathbf{d}_{\mathrm{cov}} > 0.25$). The drive term $I(m)$ adds $0.16\,g_{\mathrm{cur}}(\mathbf{u}_m^\top \mathbf{d}_{\mathrm{front}})_+ + 0.18\,g_{\mathrm{obj}}(\mathbf{u}_m^\top \mathbf{d}_{\mathrm{obj}})_+ + 0.20(\hat{A}_m - 0.5)$ (plus a flank bonus when the objective lies behind the threat) when not exposed, and an escape bonus $0.25 + 0.25(-\mathbf{u}_m^\top \mathbf{d}_{\mathrm{thr}})_+ + 0.20(\mathbf{u}_m^\top \mathbf{d}_{\mathrm{cov}})_+$ for escape candidates when exposed. The stagnation term $G(m)$ penalizes idle or stationary candidates by $1.15\,\sigma$ and rewards moving candidates by up to $0.30\,\sigma$ when spatial stagnation $\sigma > 0.20$. The survival threshold $\theta_{\mathrm{surv}}$ is the evolved gene value shifted by a bounded strategic-caution offset $\in [-0.04, 0.06]$. If, after all terms, the best candidate is still `Idle` under exposure, the dedicated escape candidate `M4` is raised $0.75$ above the maximum. With the shield disabled, the escape exemption and anti-freeze veto are removed while the survival penalty applies to all candidates. Exempting $\mathrm{esc}_m$ ensures that when surrounded—where all candidates face depressed raw survival—the world model penalizes reckless advances without suppressing retreat.

Concurrently at $60\text{ Hz}$, `BasalGanglia3DTimingEngineV2` gates shot/interaction commitment via a Direct (`Go`) vs. Hyperdirect (`STN-SNr`) bounded-urgency accumulator [@frank2006hold; @cisek2009urgency]:

$$A_{t+\Delta t} = \mathrm{clip}\!\left(A_t + r_{\mathrm{int}} \Delta t \left[ D_{\mathrm{Go}} + 1.15\,\frac{\tau_{\mathrm{wait}}}{T_{\max}} - \sum_{j=1}^4 g_j f_j - B_{\mathrm{ctx}} \right],\, 0,\, 1.5\right), \tag{9}$$

opening the gate when $A_t \ge 0.72$ or $\tau_{\mathrm{wait}} \ge T_{\max}$ ($260\text{ ms}$ exposed; $750\text{ ms}$ in refuge), with dopamine-plastic gains $g_j$ over crossfire risk $f_1$, ballistic lead-sync $f_2$, `S1`–`GWT`/metacognitive conflict $f_3$, and epistemic fog $f_4$. Two structural guards prevent pathological braking in Eq. (9): first, ballistic lead-sync braking $f_2 = 1 - p_{\mathrm{opp}}$ is gated to zero whenever the agent executes `M1` (`Frontier/Turf Paint`) or no dynamic target/crossfire is present ($p_{\mathrm{opp}} \le 0.16 \land \mathrm{risk}_{\mathrm{kin}} \le 0.05$), preventing empty-floor territory painting from waiting on nonexistent enemy lead lock; second, `is_bg_emergency_escape_exempt` grants **instant $0\text{ ms}$ bypass** whenever `EvadeHazard` is selected or open-space danger exceeds $\mathrm{hazard}_t \ge 0.42$ (or $\mathrm{risk}_{\mathrm{kin}} \ge 0.48$).

> **Proposition 2 (Bounded Deliberation Delay and Zero-Latency Emergency Escape):** Conditioned on simulator weapon-cooldown readiness (`can_commit_now == True`), for any finite brake $B_{\mathrm{STN}} < \infty$ in Eq. (9), internal basal ganglia deliberation delay $\tau_{\mathrm{wait}}$ is upper-bounded by $\tau_{\mathrm{wait}} \le T_{\max}$ ($260\text{ ms}$ outside refuge; $750\text{ ms}$ inside refuge) via the hard deadline disjunction $A_t \ge 0.72 \lor \tau_{\mathrm{wait}} \ge T_{\max}$. Moreover, whenever `disable_bg_escape_bypass == False` and either $v_{m^\star} = \text{EvadeHazard}$ or $(\neg\text{in\_refuge} \land (\mathrm{hazard}_t \ge 0.42 \lor \mathrm{risk}_{\mathrm{kin}} \ge 0.48))$, pre-accumulator short-circuiting sets $g_{\mathrm{commit}} = 1$ with $\tau_{\mathrm{wait}} = 0\text{ ms}$, preventing mutual deadlock between survival vetoing (Eq. 8) and basal ganglia braking (Eq. 9).

### 4.3 Metastable Global Workspace & Epistemic-Only Top-Down Loops (Resolving P4)

#### Broadcast-Occupancy Habituation and Phasic Workspace Ignition
Five cortical specialists project into `ActiveGWTWorkspace3D` ($\mathbb{R}^{128}$) [@baars1988gwt; @vanrullen2021deep; @goyal2022coordination]: Perceptual (`P`: foveal latent + object slots), Spatial (`I`: MEC + HD + compass), World Model (`W`: RSSM deterministic + stochastic state), Somatic/Motor (`M`: proprioception + hazard), and Strategic/Self (`S`: `StrategicTransformer3D` episodic context). To counter Pathology P4 (a workspace whose leading specialist rarely changes), pre-softmax competition scores $e_k$ incorporate an exponential moving-average broadcast-occupancy fatigue trace $\bar{\alpha}_k \in [0, 1]$ (functionally analogous to short-term synaptic depression; [@tsodyks1997neural]) together with a phasic novelty boost driven by fresh modality-specific prediction error $\nu_k$:

$$\tilde{e}_k = e_k - 2.45\,\mathrm{ReLU}(\bar{\alpha}_k - 0.20)\exp(-1.6\,\nu_k) + 1.15\tanh(\nu_k), \qquad \boldsymbol{\alpha}_t = \mathrm{softmax}(1.6\,\tilde{\mathbf{e}}_t). \tag{10}$$

Global ignition $\iota_t \in (0, 1)$ combines specialist coalition coherence $C_t$, workspace vector shift $\Delta_{\mathrm{ws}} = \mathrm{clip}(1.1(1 - \cos(\bar{\mathbf{w}}_t, \mathbf{w}_{t-1})), 0, 1)$, and peak phasic novelty $\max_k \tanh(\nu_k)$ through a steep sigmoid gate ($\kappa \approx 12$), modulating reverberant workspace retention $\mathbf{w}_t = \mathrm{LN}((1 - g_w)\mathbf{w}_{t-1} + g_w \sum_k \alpha_{t,k}\mathbf{u}_{t,k})$ with $g_w = 0.12 + 0.76\,\iota_t$. Steady cruising thus maintains moderate reverberant working memory ($\iota_t \approx 0.22\text{--}0.28$), whereas unexpected threats or prediction errors trigger phasic ignition spikes and rapid specialist handoffs.

#### Gain-Conserving, Hallucination-Free Top-Down Feedback ($t-1 \to t$)
Workspace state $\mathbf{w}_{t-1}$ and world-model state $\mathbf{h}_{t-1}$ close three top-down loops at step $t$ while strictly respecting **physical map immutability**. First, recurrent thalamocortical modulation splits $0.30\,\mathbf{z}_{\mathrm{td}}^{t-1}$ into pre-workspace perceptual encoding $\mathbf{z}_{\mathrm{perc}}^t = \tanh(\mathbf{z}_t + \Delta\mathbf{z}_{\mathrm{slot}} + 0.30\,\mathbf{z}_{\mathrm{td}}^{t-1})$ and $0.70\,\mathbf{z}_{\mathrm{td}}^t$ post-ignition ($0.30 + 0.70 = 1.00$, preventing runaway feedback amplification). Second, `ObjectSlotPredictiveCortex3D` separates background turf (`Slot 0`) from foreground entities (`Slots 1..3`) [@locatello2020slot]; top-down priors shift initial slot queries via $\mathbf{q}_{\mathrm{slot}} \leftarrow \mathbf{q}_0 + 0.45\tanh(\gamma_{\mathrm{td}})\tanh(f_{\mathrm{td}}(\mathbf{w}_{t-1}, \mathbf{h}_{t-1}))$ and scale retinotopic feature gain $\mathbf{G}_{16} \in [0.50, 1.60]^{16\times 16}$, with $\gamma_{\mathrm{td}}$ initialized to $0$ so legacy checkpoints load with exact $0.0$ perturbation. Third, top-down surprise and `StrategicTransformer3D` gaze lookahead $\Delta\mathbf{x}_{\mathrm{focus}}$ increase `volumetric_fog` ($8\times 4\times 8$) in uninspected target sectors—biasing Superior-Colliculus active gaze scanning *where to look next* without ever writing fake walls or fake paint into `occ_state`, `surface_map`, or Kalman localization.

### 4.4 Metacognitive Self-Model, `Plan-A` Inner Speech, & Offline Sleep (Resolving P5)

#### Higher-Order Efference-Copy Self-Model
`CoarseSelfModel3D` [@lau2011hot; @graziano2013social] encodes current internal-somatic state $\mathbf{m}_t = \tanh(f_{\mathrm{enc}}(\mathbf{w}_t, \tilde{\mathbf{p}}_t) + \tanh(\gamma_{\mathrm{sch}})\mathbf{W}_{\mathrm{sch}}\boldsymbol{\alpha}_t) \in \mathbb{R}^8$ and predicts it from $\mathbf{m}_{t-1}$ plus an efference copy of the previous chosen action and attention schema $[\boldsymbol{\phi}_{t-1}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}_{t-1}]$. High self-prediction error $u_{\mathrm{self}} = \frac{1}{8}\|\mathrm{sg}[\mathbf{m}_t] - \hat{\mathbf{m}}_t\|_2^2$ lowers the workspace ignition threshold, feeds a 9D metacognitive residual into `StrategicTransformer3D`, widens non-crisis Boltzmann exploration temperature, and contributes to basal ganglia conflict braking.

#### `Plan-A` Motor-Isolated Grounded Inner Speech
`OnlineProtoSymbolRVQ3D` quantizes $[\mathbf{w}_t, \mathbf{m}_t, \mathbf{h}_t]$ into 32 coarse symbols $\times$ 8 residual modifiers (`◆00..◆31`), which condition `EndogenousBrocaWernickeCortex` to emit short tactical utterances grounded against foreground visual slots (`Slots 1..3`) vs. background hard negatives (`Slot 0`) via symmetric `InfoNCE` + foreground `MIL-InfoNCE` [@miech2020milnce]. To prevent Pathology P5 (verbal action freezing), **Invariant 5 (`Plan-A` Isolation)** holds `allow_broca_motor_bias = False` by default, enforcing identically zero additive bias from the Broca token stream on candidate Q-values:

$$\max_{m \in \{0,\dots,7\}} \left| Q^{\mathrm{Plan\text{-}A}}_{\mathrm{Broca\text{-}ON}}(s_t, m) - Q^{\mathrm{Plan\text{-}A}}_{\mathrm{Broca\text{-}OFF}}(s_t, m) \right| \equiv 0.0. \tag{11}$$

Invariant 5 isolates generated language, not the underlying symbol code: the RVQ proto-symbol prior $b_{\mathrm{proto}}$ still enters $Q_{\mathrm{fused}}$ with weight $0.15$ (Sec. 4.2) as a non-verbal habit prior.

#### Continual Anchor Rollback and Single-GPU PBT + 4-Phase Sleep (`S-1..S-4`)
Online updates (`batch = 16` prioritized transitions every 8 recorded steps; PER $\alpha = 0.6$ with $\beta$ annealed from $0.45$ toward $1.0$; sleep minibatches of `24`) strictly separate 1-step sensory outcomes $s_{t+1}$ (supervising visual predictive coding) from 3-step returns ($\gamma = 0.96$) at $s_{t+3}$ (supervising Double-DQN) and contiguous 4-step trajectory chunks (supervising open-loop `RSSM` rollouts), regularized toward the best rolling-score anchor via `L2-SP` ($1.2 \times 10^{-4}$; [@li2018explicit]). If any step produces non-finite tensors, $|Q|_{\max} > 50.0$, $|\delta_{\mathrm{TD}}|_{\max} > 65.0$, or $|\theta|_{\max} > 10^3$, `_emergency_rollback_to_anchor` immediately restores clean anchor weights and purges AdamW momentum (`optimizer.state.clear()`).

Across generations, `arena_pbt_trainer.py` multiplexes 4 evolved hyperparameter archetypes (`ObjectiveStriker`, `TurfController`, `StealthInterceptor`, `BalancedTactician`) and frozen Hall-of-Fame checkpoints onto one `PopGPUServer` via Prioritized Fictitious Self-Play ($w_i = \max(0.05, 4 p_i(1 - p_i))$ around $50\%$ Elo win probability; [@vinyals2019alphastar]), interleaving generations with 4-phase offline sleep [@diekelmann2010sleep; @tononi2014shyh]:
1. **`S-1` (NREM Sharp-Wave Ripple Replay)**: Clean prioritized replay (`dreaming_noise = 0.0`) over high-TD/high-surprise transitions.
2. **`S-2` (Cortical-Semantic Consolidation)**: Mildly perturbed (`noise = 0.02`) strategic mode and multimodal symbol grounding updates.
3. **`S-3` (REM Counterfactual Dreaming)**: Latent-perturbed (`noise = 0.08`) multi-step imagination and encoder-detached counterfactual exposure de-confounding.
4. **`S-4` (Synaptic Homeostasis & Distillation)**: Global $0.9995\times$ multiplicative downscaling on active $\ge 2\text{D}$ weight matrices plus a $0.15$ Polyak target-network consolidation step.
