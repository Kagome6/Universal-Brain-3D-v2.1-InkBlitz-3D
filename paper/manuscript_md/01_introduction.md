## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 The Real-Time Integration Problem in Embodied 3D Cognition

An embodied agent acting in a dynamic, partially observable 3D arena must satisfy competing temporal and epistemic demands at the same time. At the $16.7\text{ ms}$ ($60\text{ Hz}$) sensorimotor timescale, the body must track moving targets under gravity, strafe around obstacles while aiming in an independent direction, and dive into cover without frame stalls. At intermediate timescales ($40\text{--}100\text{ ms}$, $10\text{--}24\text{ Hz}$), the agent must remember terrain and territorial paint states outside its narrow camera frustum, imagine multi-step consequences of candidate maneuvers, inhibit premature shots into crossfire, and redirect attention when expectations fail. Across episodes and generations, the agent must consolidate sparse tactical discoveries into fast reflexes without diverging numerically or remaining permanently dependent on hand-crafted heuristic priors.

Standard end-to-end deep reinforcement learning handles this by training a single feed-forward or recurrent policy network mapping observations directly to actions [@mnih2015dqn; @vinyals2019alphastar]. While effective when massive parallel simulation is available, a monolithic policy couples motor latency to total network depth, obscures internal failure modes, and lacks explicit mechanisms for mid-episode spatial re-anchoring—for instance, retaining discovered stage layout and territory control after being eliminated and returned to a spawn point. Conversely, classical symbolic and hybrid cognitive architectures such as ACT-R [@anderson2004actr], Soar [@laird2012soar], LIDA [@franklin2014lida], and large-scale neural cognitive models such as Spaun [@eliasmith2012spaun] formalize modular workspaces and memory systems, yet rarely operate directly on 60 Hz 3D continuous-angle ballistic combat and visual predictive coding with modern deep learning components.

### 1.2 Research Positioning: Between Anatomical Fidelity and Standalone Primitives

Contemporary brain-inspired and cognitive AI research largely divides into three approaches, each optimizing a different constraint. Biophysical microcircuit simulations, exemplified by the Blue Brain / Human Brain Project [@markram2015reconstruction], reconstruct cellular morphology, ion-channel kinetics, and spiking microcircuits; while directly informative for cellular neurophysiology, simulating Hodgkin–Huxley or dense spiking networks at whole-brain scale cannot run a closed 60 Hz 3D tactical loop with online learning on a single consumer workstation. Anatomical Brain Reference Architectures (WBA / WBRA) [@yamakawa2021wba] propose building general intelligence by mapping software modules onto mesoscopic neuroanatomical circuits so each component preserves biological input-output semantics; however, requiring strict subnuclear circuit isomorphism across cortex, thalamus, hippocampus, and basal ganglia *prior* to closing the end-to-end 3D sensorimotor loop risks stalling system-level integration. Meanwhile, task-driven deep reinforcement learning and representation learning have produced reliable algorithmic building blocks, including Dueling Double Q-Networks with Prioritized Experience Replay [@mnih2015dqn; @vanhasselt2016double; @wang2016dueling; @schaul2016per], discrete latent world models (`DreamerV2`/`V3`) [@hafner2021dreamerv2; @hafner2023dreamerv3], object-centric `Slot Attention` [@locatello2020slot], shared global workspace bottlenecks [@vanrullen2021deep; @goyal2022coordination], contrastive and quantized representations (`InfoNCE`, `MIL-NCE`, `VQ-VAE`, `RVQ`) [@vandenoord2018cpc; @miech2020milnce; @vandenoord2017vqvae; @zeghidour2021soundstream], and competitive multi-agent leagues (`PBT`/`FTW`, `PFSP`) [@jaderberg2017pbt; @jaderberg2019ftw; @vinyals2019alphastar]. In practice, these components are typically studied either in isolation or inside a single-rate agent loop rather than wired together as concurrent, multi-rate functional brain regions with explicit cross-module safety and gradient-isolation boundaries.

**Universal Brain 3D (v2.3.0)** targets the middle ground between mesoscopic cognitive decomposition and modern deep RL primitives (Table 1). At the macro-architectural level, we adopt WBA-style functional separation across cortical and subcortical loops running at distinct frequencies ($60\text{ Hz}$, $24\text{ Hz}$, $12\text{ Hz}$, event-driven, and inter-episode offline sleep). Inside each region, rather than simulating raw biological microcircuits, we instantiate established machine learning and robotics primitives by functional role:
- **Landmark Kalman filtering & 4-layer field mapping** ($60\text{ Hz}$ self-localization, obstacle/elevation memory, and decaying territory belief),
- **`TT-D3QN` (Tensor-Train Dueling Double Q-Network)** ($24\text{ Hz}$ fast reflexive scoring over spatially cleared 6-DoF maneuvers),
- **`Slot Attention` & a 3-head categorical `RSSM`** ($12\text{ Hz}$ foreground object binding and 3-step counterfactual reward/survival/advance imagination),
- **`ActiveGWT` & a causal `Transformer`** ($12\text{ Hz}$ 5-specialist global workspace competition and multi-step episodic strategic context),
- **`RVQ` (Residual Vector Quantization) & `InfoNCE`/`MIL-InfoNCE`** (discrete tactical proto-symbols and motor-isolated `Plan-A` inner speech), and
- **`PBT`/`PFSP` with progressive scaffold annealing & offline replay** (single-GPU competitive league evolution, curriculum removal of hand-crafted topological priors, and 4-phase sleep consolidation).

Rather than claiming novelty for any individual layer, we evaluate whether the assembled parts function as a single coherent intelligence across three architectural criteria: *replaceability* (plug-compatibility under module swaps), *integration without interference* (shared state routing free of multi-objective gradient cancellation, zero-gate saddle deadlocks, or silent passengers), and *experimentability* (measurable multi-metric gains and trade-offs as organs are added cumulatively and hand-crafted scaffolds are annealed to zero).

### 1.3 Five Integration Pathologies Encountered in Closed-Loop Assembly

The central finding of our engineering effort is that placing established perceptual, memory, world-model, workspace, and language models into a shared 60 Hz 3D loop **fails if connected naively**. Specifically, five cross-module failure modes emerge during live closed-loop operation:

1. **Pathology P1 (Multi-Rate Blocking & GPU Context Duplication)**: Calling a multi-step world model synchronously inside the 60 Hz control thread (`>30 ms` per step with 15-bipartition $\Phi_G$ estimation) violates the $16.67\text{ ms}$ frame budget, while spawning separate Python processes that each import PyTorch duplicates multi-gigabyte CUDA contexts and exhausts Windows pagefile commit limits. Furthermore, if fast ($24\text{ Hz}$) and slow ($12\text{ Hz}$) loops emit raw continuous motor vectors asynchronously, stale commands cause oscillatory jitter near obstacles.
2. **Pathology P2 (Dynamic-Entity Localization Corruption & Respawn Amnesia)**: In competitive arenas, feeding all visible objects into localization updates allows moving opponents and carried objectives to pull self-position estimates off course. Conversely, resetting memory on mid-match elimination erases discovered wall geometry, occluded sightlines, and territory state, forcing repeated blind exploration.
3. **Pathology P3 (Observational Confounding, Encoder Gradient Conflict, & Inhibitory Miscalibration)**: In on-policy trajectories, agents execute stationary observation (`M7`) primarily when already safe behind cover, causing standard world models to confound open-space idling with high survival probability; yet naively backpropagating counterfactual or temporal-difference losses into shared visual/slot encoders induces destructive gradient interference ($\cos = -0.73$ between counterfactual and visual losses, and $-0.33$ between TD and world-model losses; [@yu2020pcgrad]). Simultaneously, an unconditioned basal ganglia `STN-SNr` brake [@frank2006hold] either delays emergency evasion under fire or falsely throttles empty-floor territory painting, while permanent hand-crafted compass priors can mask whether neural valuation has actually learned autonomous competence.
4. **Pathology P4 (Global Workspace Stagnation, Zero-Gate Saddle Deadlock, & Top-Down Hallucination)**: When heterogeneous specialists compete via soft attention [@baars1988gwt; @dehaene2011experimental] without novelty-driven habituation, the broadcast becomes nearly uniform and rarely changes its leading specialist. Conversely, when new top-down feedback pathways ($t-1 \to t$) are attached via standard zero-initialized `tanh(0) * u` residual gates for backward compatibility, exact zero initialization creates a bilinear saddle point ($\nabla_u = 0$) that leaves upstream specialist pathways permanently dormant. Moreover, if top-down workspace predictions write directly into spatial occupancy grids, transient imagination errors fabricate permanent phantom walls.
5. **Pathology P5 (Verbal Perseveration & Online Continual Drift)**: Allowing endogenous inner-speech tokens [@vygotsky1986thought] to bias fast action Q-values creates self-reinforcing verbal loops (emitting *"hold"* tokens that suppress movement, keeping the sensory scene unchanged so *"hold"* is emitted again). Meanwhile, online TD + world-model updates during adversarial self-play risk Q-value spikes or `NaN` gradients that destroy accumulated competence.

### 1.4 Summary of Contributions

The central contribution of this paper is **neither a new standalone neural layer nor a state-of-the-art game win-rate claim**; rather, it is a **60 Hz whole-brain integration microkernel, five formal coupling contracts resolving Pathologies P1–P5, and a falsifiable integration audit benchmark**. Crucially, even modules whose behavioral contribution is regime-dependent or neutral—such as motor-isolated `Plan-A` inner speech or inter-generation sleep—are intentionally included so we can test whether slow symbolic, diagnostic, and consolidative loops can coexist inside a 60 Hz single-GPU loop without violating real-time deadlines, corrupting shared representations, or inducing behavioral lock-in:

1. **Single-GPU Multi-Rate Cognitive Microkernel (`Barrier 1` & Candidate Currency, Sec. 3)**: A 7-process OS architecture communicating over 64-byte-aligned double-buffered `SeqLock + CRC32` shared memory, where strictly one process owns PyTorch/CUDA and `SpatialTimingProcess` ($60\text{ Hz}$) publishes an authoritative 8-candidate 6-DoF pose set evaluated coherently across $24\text{ Hz}$ and $12\text{ Hz}$ loops.
2. **Allocentric 4-Layer Spatial Memory with Respawn Re-Anchoring & Decoupled Ballistic Control (Sec. 4.1)**: Stationary-landmark-only Kalman localization paired with an epistemically decaying `48x48` territory/elevation map that preserves stage knowledge across mid-episode respawns (`flags |= 16`), evaluated across both open (`Stage 1`) and centrally occluded (`Stage 2`) arena geometries so topological trade-offs are exposed transparently (Sec. 6.3).
3. **World-Model Encoder Ownership, Counterfactual Shielding, & Progressive Scaffold Annealing (Sec. 4.2)**: Joint arbitration across `TT-D3QN`, a dopamine-plastic `STN-SNr` urgency race with target-gated braking and $0\text{ ms}$ emergency escape bypass, and a 3-head (`horizon=3`) categorical `RSSM` that exclusively owns visual/slot encoder representation learning while an encoder-detached counterfactual penalty [@alshiekh2018safe; @yu2020pcgrad] and progressive `Scaffold Annealing` ($\lambda_{\mathrm{scaf}}: 1 \to 0$) transition the agent to autonomous neural arbitration.
4. **Workspace Habituation, Straight-Through Zero-Gate Awakening, `Plan-A` Inner Speech, & Online $\Phi_G$ Diagnostics (Sec. 4.3–5)**: A novelty-and-occupancy habituation circuit in `ActiveGWTWorkspace3D`, shadow-tangent straight-through zero-gates (`apply_zero_gate`) breaking initial saddle deadlocks on $t-1 \to t$ epistemic feedback loops without corrupting physical maps, Broca-isolated inner speech, 4-phase inter-generation sleep (`S-1..S-4`), and a debiased off-diagonal-ridge estimator of lagged causal integrated information ($\Phi_G$; [@oizumi2016unified]).
5. **Clean-Room Two-Stage 3D Testbed and Integration Audit Protocol (Sec. 2 & 6; Table 2)**: An open-source symmetric arena (**InkBlitz 3D** in Unity 6 plus a pure-NumPy/C++ counterpart featuring `Stage 1: Classic Citadel` and `Stage 2: Occluded Twin-Flank`), $K = 3$ independent multi-thousand-update self-play runs, held-out interventional simulator rollouts, and a three-criteria audit of replaceability, gradient interference, link sensitivity, and cumulative organ scaling that reports null and negative results alongside positive gains.
